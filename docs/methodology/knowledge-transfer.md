# Methodology: Knowledge-Transfer Engineering

> "Knowledge stored only in the mind of the founder is an existential vulnerability. Knowledge that is not taught to successors dies with the body."

---

## 1. The Cascading Apprenticeship Model

Knowledge transfer operates through a self-replicating multi-tier structure:

```
[ Tier 0: The Prophet / Mission Founder ]
             |
             v (Personal intensive tutoring of 12 master practitioners)
[ Tier 1: Master Practitioners & System Architects ]
             |
             v (Regional workshops training 10 apprentices each)
[ Tier 2: Certified Workshop Instructors & Scribes ]
             |
             v (Community schools and municipal guilds)
[ Tier 3: Autonomous Guild Craftsmen & Civic Technicians ]
```

### The Rule of Three (Pedagogical Verification)
A candidate has not truly mastered a capability until they have successfully taught another person to demonstrate it independently:
1. **Learn**: Study the principle, participate in execution, record measurements.
2. **Demonstrate**: Build or execute the capability with zero assistance from the instructor.
3. **Teach**: Guide a new student to successful demonstration without intervening.

---

## 2. Redundancy and Medium Durability

To ensure knowledge survives dynastic wars, fires, and floods:
- **Distributed Archives**: All engineering schemas, mathematical tables, and medical protocols must be stored in duplicate across at least three geographically separate cities.
- **Durable Media**: Transition from perishable papyrus rolls to kilned ceramic cylinders, stone-inscribed public stelae, and acid-free rag paper.
