# Methodology: Ethics and Governance

> "A technological society built on slavery, fear, and secrecy is brittle. The Prophet Project designs institutions for voluntary cooperation, scientific truth, and distributed power."

---

## 1. Ethical Constraints on Labor and Development

1. **Ban on Coercive Enslavement**: Technological acceleration must never be used as a moral justification for chattel slavery, corvée abuse, or forced labor camps. Sustainable productivity arises from voluntary compensation, skill development, and worker agency.
2. **Occupational Safety Standards**: Chemical toxicity (arsenic, lead, mercury), quarry collapse hazards, and furnace burns must be actively managed with protective equipment, ventilation, and fair compensation for injuries.
3. **Equitable Surplus Distribution**: Increased agricultural yields and mechanical productivity must visibly improve the living conditions of the workers producing them, preventing elite capture and peasant rebellion.

---

## 2. Institutional Governance and Succession

- **Separation of Inspection from Execution**: Technical inspectors and accountants must report independently of the workshop managers whose work they audit.
- **Protected Dissent and Correction**: Apprentices and citizens must be legally protected when pointing out measurement errors, safety hazards, or failed predictions.
- **Succession by Merit and Examination**: Institutional leadership passes through rigorous blind practical examinations, not dynastic heredity or personal favoritism.
