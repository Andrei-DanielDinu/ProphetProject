# Methodology: Evidence and Confidence Standard

> "Distinguish what is measured from what is calculated, what is calculated from what is estimated, and what is estimated from what is wished."

---

## 1. Epistemic Confidence Categories

Every claim, dataset, and technology dossier in the Prophet Project is tagged with an explicit confidence level:

| Confidence Level | Description | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Verified** | Supported by direct archaeological, experimental, or peer-reviewed engineering data. | Replicated in peer-reviewed journals, verified archaeological excavation reports, or industrial standards (e.g., ASME, ISO). |
| **Calculated** | Derived through physical conservation equations and verified material balances. | Proven via thermodynamic, mechanical, or stoichiometric formulas with explicit input assumptions. |
| **Estimated** | Historical or geological estimates based on proxy data and analog models. | Based on comparable pre-industrial societies, geological surveys, or historical wage/yield records. |
| **Speculative** | Working hypotheses, theoretical proposals, or unverified scenarios. | Explicitly flagged as unverified; requires experimental or archaeological review before adoption. |

---

## 2. Citation and Provenance Requirements

- Every material parameter (yield strength, thermal conductivity, fuel consumption rate) must cite its empirical source.
- Ancient geological accessibility must reflect **historical surface occurrences and ancient mining depths**, not modern open-pit copper or coal operations that require giant diesel excavators and explosives.
