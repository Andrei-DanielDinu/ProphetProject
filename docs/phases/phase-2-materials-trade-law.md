# Phase 2: Materials, Trade, Law, and Infrastructure (Years 100–500)

> "A resource in the ground is not an industrial capability. Capability is the organized chain of prospecting, extraction, reduction, long-distance transport, testing, and contractual governance."

---

## 1. Phase Charter

| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Elapsed Time** | Years 100 to 500 |
| **Primary Objective** | Establish multi-regional supply networks for metals, timber, and fuel; create codified commercial law and dispute courts; engineer permanent transport infrastructure; transition from unalloyed copper to standardized tin-bronze metallurgy. |
| **Primary Constraints** | Extreme tin scarcity in Egypt; overland donkey caravan weight limits; pirate raids on maritime trade; political fragmentation during dynastic transitions. |
| **Exit Condition** | Redundant international supply chains supplying tin, copper, and cedar; standard contract law and independent commercial tribunals; paved slipways, canal bypasses, and all-weather desert road stations; established artisan guilds with hereditary apprenticeships. |

---

## 2. Priority Workstreams

### A. Metallurgy and Material Quality Control
- **Tin Procurement Networks**: Establish trade pacts and maritime trading posts reaching Anatolian, Aegean, and Cornish tin corridors. Maintain strategic tin stockpiles in fortified coastal depots.
- **Bronze Standardization**: Enforce standardized alloy grades:
  - Cutting Tools & Saws: $10\text{--}12\%\text{ Sn}$ (maximum hardness and edge retention).
  - Bearings & Bushings: $8\text{--}10\%\text{ Sn}$ (low friction and high toughness).
  - Statuary & Vessels: $4\text{--}6\%\text{ Sn}$ with trace lead for high casting fluidity.
- **Refractory Kilns**: Construct double-chamber downdraft kilns firing ceramics up to $1150^\circ\text{C}$, producing vitrified stoneware and high-temperature smelting crucibles.

### B. Civil Engineering and Transport Infrastructure
- **Canal Bypass Locks**: Construct stone-faced flash locks and spillways to allow riverboats to navigate Nile rapids and cataracts safely.
- **Desert Caravan Stations**: Fortify wadi routes (e.g., Wadi Hammamat to the Red Sea) with deep stone-lined cisterns spaced every 25 kilometers (one day's donkey travel), complete with permanent guard garrisons and grain caches.
- **Maritime Ship Construction**: Transition from papyrus skiffs to mortise-and-tenon cedar sailing ships with central keels, steering oars, and square-rigged sails capable of navigating the Eastern Mediterranean and Red Sea.

### C. Legal Codes and Commercial Institutions
- **Codified Contract Law**: Inscribe standard contracts for loans, apprenticeships, vessel charters, and partnership ventures.
- **Commercial Dispute Resolution**: Establish merchant courts independent of the royal mortuary administration to resolve contractual disputes, enforcing standard weights and interest rates.
- **Worker Compensation and Safety**: Codify employer liability for quarry injuries, providing grain stipends and medical care for wounded laborers.

---

## 3. Exit Criteria Evaluation

1. **Supply Chain Redundancy**: If any single tin or timber route is cut by war, alternative routes or stockpiles can supply metal workshops for at least 10 years.
2. **Standardization**: Trade contracts across multiple cities use identical units of value and volume.
3. **Legal Continuity**: Commercial courts enforce contracts reliably across successive royal reigns without arbitrary asset confiscation.
