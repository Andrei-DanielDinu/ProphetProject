# Phase 0: Arrival and Legitimacy (Years 0–5)

> "First, survive. Then, become useful. Then, build something that can outlive you."

---

## 1. Phase Charter

| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Elapsed Time** | Years 0 to 5 |
| **Primary Objective** | Establish personal survival, master local language and customs, earn social legitimacy through humble utility, and build the first teaching workshop. |
| **Baseline Setting** | Egypt c. 2975 BCE (First Dynasty, reigns of Hor-Aha / Djer / Djet). |
| **Primary Constraints** | Zero modern equipment; physical vulnerability; risk of being treated as an eccentric, foreign spy, or sorcerer; dependence on local patronage. |
| **Exit Condition** | A protected physical workshop-school operating under recognized local authority; a first cohort of apprentices capable of replicating basic measurement and craft techniques; multiple independent institutional relationships. |

---

## 2. The 72-Hour Survival Protocol

Upon physical arrival, the Prophet possesses memory and theoretical understanding, but zero physical possessions, clothing, tools, or followers.

1. **Environmental Assessment**: Locate clean freshwater (Nile or active wadi springs). Avoid direct desert solar exposure during peak daylight hours. Assess terrain and navigate toward agricultural boundary settlements.
2. **First Contact Strategy**: Approach small village farming clusters or craft workshops rather than fortified royal estates or military patrols. Present oneself peacefully as a traveler seeking shelter in exchange for labor.
3. **Linguistic Immersion**: Prioritize high-frequency utility nouns and verbs: water, bread, fire, barley, copper, wood, chisel, measure, count, sick, hurt, yes, no, who, where. Map spoken phonetic roots to visible objects and daily work routines.
4. **Demonstrating Modest Value**: Offer immediate, non-threatening, verifiable improvements:
   - Calculating field areas or crop volume tallies without mathematical errors.
   - Constructing a true right-angle triangle (3-4-5 knotted rope) for field surveying.
   - Sharpening or repairing wood and stone tools using controlled pressure flaking or whetstone grinding.
   - Demonstrating hygienic wound cleaning with clean boiled water and fresh linen.

---

## 3. Transition to Formal Institutional Role (Years 1–3)

Avoid claiming divine status or attempting a political coup. The historical Early Dynastic Egyptian state was deeply conservative, organized around kingship, royal mortuary estates, and temple priesthoods.

- **Acceptable Historical Personas**: Master Surveyor, Royal Scribe of Accounts, Workshop Overseer, Physician's Assistant.
- **Diversifying Patronage**: Cultivate relationships with local nomarchs (regional governors), estate overseers, and master metal smiths. Never become the exclusive property or secret asset of a single court official, as factional shifts or dynastic deaths could leave the project defenseless.

---

## 4. Establishing the First Workshop-School (Years 3–5)

1. **Physical Compound**: Secure an enclosed mudbrick compound with clean water access, storage for charcoal and clay, and a sheltered scribal courtyard.
2. **Cohort Selection**: Select 6 to 12 inquisitive young apprentices: scribal students, potters, and coppersmiths.
3. **Core Curriculum**:
   - Standard units of measure (Royal Cubit, Deben weight, balance beam operation).
   - Base-10 arithmetic, fractions, geometry, and error tracking.
   - Observation and record-keeping on papyrus, leather rolls, and wooden tablets.
   - Physical demonstrations: crucible copper smelting, ceramic kiln drafts, seed selection trials.
4. **Redundant Documentation**: All lessons and measured observations are recorded in duplicate and stored in separate ceramic jars at different locations.

---

## 5. Phase 0 Exit Verification Checklist

- [x] Spoken fluency in Early Dynastic Egyptian sufficient for technical instruction.
- [x] Stable personal food, shelter, and legal security under provincial law.
- [x] At least three measurable improvements (surveying, accounting, grain storage) adopted voluntarily by local practitioners.
- [x] First apprentice cohort demonstrates independent ability to calibrate scales and smelt copper without the Prophet's intervention.
- [x] Redundant archives established in at least two distinct settlements.
