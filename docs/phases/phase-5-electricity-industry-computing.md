# Phase 5: Electricity, Communications, Modern Industry, and Computing (Years 2200–3200)

> "Electricity turns energy into a liquid network distributed across continents; the telegraph and radio make information instantaneous; catalytic high-pressure chemistry captures atmospheric nitrogen to feed billions; and early computing automates mathematical logic."

---

## 1. Phase Charter

| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Elapsed Time** | Years 2200 to 3200 |
| **Primary Objective** | Construct regional high-voltage polyphase AC electrical grids; establish transcontinental wired telegraphy and transoceanic radio communications; scale Haber-Bosch ammonia synthesis; invent thermionic vacuum tubes and stored-program electronic computers. |
| **Primary Constraints** | High-conductivity electrolytic copper wire drawing and dielectric insulation; extreme pressure/temperature embrittlement during ammonia synthesis; vacuum tube filament burnouts. |
| **Exit Condition** | Interconnected high-voltage AC electrical grids powering industry; global telecommunication networks operating over cable and radio; synthetic nitrogen fertilizers feeding expanding populations; electronic digital computers executing complex scientific simulations. |

---

## 2. Priority Workstreams

### A. Electrical Generation, Transmission, and Motors
- **AC Polyphase Dynamos and Induction Motors**: Build Nikola Tesla polyphase induction motors and multi-megawatt steam turbine generators with laminated silicon-steel cores.
- **High-Voltage Step-Up Transformers**: Step voltages up to 110–345 kV to drop line current by 25–100x, reducing $I^2 R$ transmission line losses by factors of hundreds across regional grids.
- **Dielectric Insulation**: Insulate conductors with glazed porcelain suspension bells, vulcanized rubber, and oil-cooled paper wrappings.

### B. Global Telecommunications
- **Electromagnetic Telegraphy**: Lay copper-core cables insulated with gutta-percha across ocean seabeds; decode signals using Morse code and polarized sensitive galvanometers.
- **Telephony and Audio Amplification**: Deploy carbon microphones and inductive induction coils.
- **Radio Waves and Superheterodyne Receivers**: Generate high-frequency carrier waves using spark gaps, Poulsen arcs, and vacuum tube oscillators; modulate RF signals for transoceanic wireless telegraphy and broadcast voice communications.

### C. Industrial Chemistry and Nitrogen Fixation
- **The Haber-Bosch Process**: React stoichiometric nitrogen (from air liquefaction) and hydrogen (from steam-methane reforming or water gas) at 200 bar and 450°C over promoted magnetite catalysts to produce anhydrous liquid ammonia.
- **Petroleum Petrochemistry**: Fractionally distill crude petroleum in bubble-cap towers, producing gasoline, diesel, lubricants, and feedstocks for synthetic polymers.

### D. Computing Precursors and Electronic Digital Machines
- **Electromechanical Relays**: Build automated telephone routing exchanges and early relay binary calculators.
- **Thermionic Vacuum Tubes**: Fabricate triodes, tetrodes, and pentodes in high-vacuum glass envelopes ($< 10^{-6}\text{ Torr}$) with barium getters for signal amplification, high-frequency oscillators, and bistable digital flip-flop circuits.
- **Stored-Program Electronic Computers**: Construct vacuum-tube and early transistorized digital computers (Von Neumann architecture) featuring binary ALUs, program counters, and magnetic core memory, capable of executing thousands of floating-point instructions per second for ballistics and weather forecasting.

## 3. Exit Criteria Evaluation

1. **Electrification**: Electric light, motor drive, and electrochemical reduction are widely available across industrial centers.
2. **Food Independence**: Synthetic nitrogen fertilizers break the Malthusian agricultural ceiling, guaranteeing stable food yields regardless of organic manure availability.
3. **Information Speed**: Scientific papers, astronomical measurements, and logistical orders transmit across continents in minutes rather than months.
