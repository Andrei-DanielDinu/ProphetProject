# Baseline: Historical Uncertainties and Methodological Caveats

> "Honest systems engineering requires admitting what archaeological evidence cannot yet prove."

---

## 1. Key Historical Chronology Gaps

1. **Exact Water-Lifting Chronology**: The counterweighted shaduf is explicitly documented in the New Kingdom (c. 1500 BCE), while the animal-driven saqiya waterwheel appears in Ptolemaic times (c. 300 BCE). The Prophet Project does not assume these mechanisms existed in 2975 BCE; they are treated as early mechanical innovations to be introduced systematically.
2. **Tin Provenance Debate**: The precise geological sources of Bronze Age Mediterranean tin remain an active subject of isotopic research (Cornwall, Anatolian Taurus mountains, or Central Asian deposits). The model treats tin as a critical logistics bottleneck with multiple alternative trade routes.
3. **Population Estimates**: Early Dynastic Egyptian population estimates range from 1.0 to 1.8 million inhabitants. Workforce calculations are parameterized to support sensitivity testing across this range.
