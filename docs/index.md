# The Prophet Project

**A field guide to becoming capable, building capability, and ensuring the work outlives its founder.**

The mission: help a community move from a low-technology starting point toward the modern technological frontier as quickly as is plausibly possible—without pretending that knowledge alone replaces materials, skilled practice, institutions, or trust.

## Choose your path

- [Become the Uomo Universale](uomo-universale.md) — cultivate the person who can start, learn, teach, and lead responsibly.
- [Technology Roadmap](technology-roadmap.md) — understand the dependency chain from basic tools to modern technology.
- [Civilization Playbook](civilization-playbook.md) — earn cooperation, adapt to local conditions, build a durable shared mission, and train successors.

## The operating principle

**Compress avoidable delay; do not hand-wave unavoidable prerequisites.** Teach the scientific method early, build useful things people can test, document every process, and transfer responsibility before it becomes a single-person bottleneck.

“Prophet” is the project’s narrative title for a mission-starter. It does not mean supernatural authority, immunity from criticism, or entitlement to rule.
