# Technology Roadmap

*A dependency-oriented route from almost no modern technology to the capabilities humanity has today.*

This is a map of capability prerequisites, not a promise that every society can follow the same schedule. The exact route depends on ecology, population, resources, existing knowledge, trade, institutions, and available time. Modern knowledge can accelerate discovery; it cannot instantly create skilled labor, precision tools, energy, or supply chains.

## How to read a step

Each future technology dossier should define: **inputs → prerequisites → process → outputs → workforce → resources → failure modes → verification → confidence**. Record what can be done locally, what requires trade, and what depends on earlier precision or energy capabilities.

## The capability ladder

### 0. Survival, trust, and observation
Secure water, food, shelter, sanitation, personal safety, and relationships. Learn language and customs. Map local skills, materials, seasons, institutions, and risks. Establish records and a habit of testing claims.

**Gate:** stable enough conditions to learn and organize without threatening immediate survival.

### 1. Measurement, numeracy, and reproducible records
Standardize practical units; teach arithmetic, ratios, geometry, counting, calendars, and bookkeeping. Build measuring tools and durable archives. Introduce controlled experiments and error tracking.

**Unlocks:** reliable construction, agriculture experiments, trade accounting, and cumulative learning.

### 2. Food systems and population health
Improve seed selection, soil management, water control, crop storage, food safety, animal care, sanitation, and basic public health. Track yields and disease patterns without assuming modern clinical capabilities.

**Unlocks:** dependable surplus, healthier workers, specialization, and more time for craft and research.

### 3. Materials, heat, and craft specialization
Develop reliable ceramics, lime, charcoal and fuel management, glass where feasible, mining, ore processing, copper/bronze/iron routes as locally supported, and material testing. Train craftspeople through apprenticeship.

**Unlocks:** durable tools, vessels, construction materials, and controlled processes.

### 4. Mechanical systems and useful power
Advance levers, pulleys, gears, screws, pumps, mills, bearings, water/wind power, and transmission. Improve transport, roads, boats, and logistics. Treat maintenance as a core capability.

**Unlocks:** greater work output, repeatable production, and machine-building experience.

### 5. Chemistry, precision, and scientific institutions
Build balances, thermometers, optical instruments, laboratory practice, chemical separation, safe acids/alkalis, process control, standards, specialist education, and research records. Establish peer review and replication.

**Unlocks:** systematic discovery, precision manufacturing, and controlled industrial processes.

### 6. Industrial heat, steam, and machine tools
Develop suitable boilers and engines only alongside metallurgy, pressure safety, machining, fuel supply, and maintenance. Build machine tools that improve their own precision. Scale mining and transport carefully.

**Unlocks:** industrial power, higher output, and a reinforcing precision-tool chain.

### 7. Electricity and modern industrial chemistry
Establish generators, motors, insulation, conductors, power distribution, measurement, communications, and industrial chemistry. Fertilizer production requires suitable feedstocks, energy, catalysts, and process safety—not merely a formula.

**Unlocks:** scalable power, electrified manufacturing, and rapid communication.

### 8. Electronics, computing, and networks
Build high-purity materials, semiconductor process control, electronics, computation, software, networking, metrology, clean environments, and reliable supply chains. Prioritize useful instrumentation and education systems before consumer complexity.

**Unlocks:** automation, digital coordination, advanced design, and global knowledge exchange.

### 9. Contemporary frontier and resilience
Develop advanced medicine and biotechnology under strong biosafety and ethics; aerospace and satellites; modern materials; advanced energy; climate and environmental monitoring; and secure information systems. Each area is a family of capabilities with extensive prerequisites.

**Gate:** the community can sustain research, production, maintenance, education, and safe governance across frontier domains.

## The compounding loop

**Better measurement → better tools → better materials and energy → better production → more surplus → broader education and research → better measurement.**

Protect this loop from bottlenecks: food, energy, skilled teachers, precision, transport, trust, and reliable institutions.

## Important limitation

A full modern capability stack cannot be responsibly reduced to a short list of inventions. The roadmap must be expanded into evidence-backed dependency records and reviewed by domain specialists. Any timeline should be scenario-specific and visibly uncertain.
