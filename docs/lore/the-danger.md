# The Lore: The Extinction Threat

> "In 2026 CE, humanity discovered an incoming terminal boundary. With six months remaining, survival was impossible. With five millennia of preparation, survival becomes an engineering challenge."

---

## 1. Threat Definition and Environmental Stress

While the specific narrative mechanism of the terminal event is configurable, it serves as a systems-engineering forcing function that shapes the development roadmap:

- **Spatial Scale**: Global biosphere disruption (e.g., massive extrasolar collision hazard, extreme stellar radiation pulse, or destabilizing deep mantle convective overturn).
- **Physical Signature**: Extreme atmospheric heating/freezing, loss of standard agricultural growing seasons, catastrophic electromagnetic and radiation pulses, and disruption of oceanic and atmospheric currents.
- **The 5,000-Year Window**: A 5,000-year development arc provides the generational runway necessary to build an advanced technological civilization equipped with space launch infrastructure, planetary observation arrays, closed-loop life support domes, and deep-space deflection systems.

---

## 2. Requirements on the Technological Stack

A civilization built merely for conquest or luxury will collapse when planetary disasters strike. The threat model requires:
1. **Planetary Monitoring**: Capability to track subatomic, seismic, atmospheric, and astronomical hazards years before arrival.
2. **Infrastructure Redundancy**: Zero single points of failure across global food, water, and power grids.
3. **Off-World Continuity**: Permanent self-sustaining off-world habitats (orbital stations, lunar settlements) ensuring human survival even if Earth's surface becomes uninhabitable.
