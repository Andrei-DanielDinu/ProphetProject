# The Lore: The Machine

> "A one-way consciousness transfer across 5,000 years: an impossible narrative premise that opens the door to rigorous physical systems engineering."

---

## 1. Operating Rules of the Narrative Premise

The Prophet Project establishes a strict boundary between its fictional transfer mechanism and its downstream engineering roadmap:

1. **One-Way Vector**: The machine cannot send physical matter, tools, microchips, books, or return messages back to 2026 CE. There is no supply line from the future.
2. **Consciousness Encoding**: The traveler's cognitive neural architecture, memory index, and procedural motor habits are transferred into a reconstructed human body at the destination coordinates.
3. **Zero Material Influx**: The Prophet arrives naked and empty-handed in the Egyptian landscape c. 2975 BCE. Every subsequent tool, nail, kiln, and written sheet must be manufactured from materials physically present in the ancient environment.
4. **Conservation Laws Apply**: Downstream of the initial arrival premise, all physical conservation laws (conservation of mass, energy, and momentum) are absolute. No perpetual motion, no free precision, and no magical materials are permitted in the engineering roadmap.
