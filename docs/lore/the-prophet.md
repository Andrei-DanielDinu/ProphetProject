# The Lore: The Prophet

> "A lone mind cast across fifty centuries carries no army, no golden treasures, and no miraculous machinery. It carries only an organized model of how the universe works, and the humility to teach it."

---

## 1. Character and Capability Profile

In the narrative framing of the Prophet Project, the traveler is not a sorcerer or divine avatar. The consciousness transfer enhances biological memory consolidation, procedural retention, and logical reasoning, but provides no supernatural powers:

- **Epistemic Modesty**: The Prophet does not claim personal omniscience. When empirical observations contradict memory, the Prophet adjusts the hypothesis rather than enforcing dogmatic belief.
- **Physical Vulnerability**: The reconstructed biological body is mortal. A crocodile bite, a contaminated water sip, or a copper spear wound will kill the Prophet just like any ancient farmer.
- **Pedagogical Calling**: The primary occupation of the Prophet is not ruler or general, but teacher and institutional architect. True success is achieved when students can teach their own students without the founder's presence.

---

## 2. Core Ethical Safeguards

To prevent the Prophet from becoming a tyrant or religious cult leader, the mission operates under four non-negotiable principles:

1. **Non-Deception**: Never claim godhood, divine bloodlines, or magical power. All demonstrations must be explained through physical principles that students can measure and replicate themselves.
2. **Voluntary Cooperation**: Never build an empire through slave labor or coercive violence. Institutional cooperation must be earned through demonstrable public benefits: cleaner water, reduced child mortality, reliable food reserves, and lighter physical toil.
3. **Transparent Auditing**: All measurements, accounts, and formulations must be open to inspection by apprentices, scribes, and local community representatives.
4. **Planned Obsolescence of the Founder**: The entire system must be designed from Year 0 to operate without the Prophet. The founder must deliberately step back from day-to-day operations to stress-test succession protocols.
