# Risk Management: Early-Warning Indicators

> "Catastrophes rarely strike without warning. They announce themselves through subtle anomalies in ledgers, river gauges, and workshop floors."

---

## 1. Leading Indicators by Domain

| Threat Domain | Subtle Early Warning Signal | Critical Threshold | Required Emergency Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Agricultural Famine** | Consecutive below-average Nilometer flood readings; grain market hoarding. | 2 consecutive years of flood $< 6\text{ m}$ at Aswan. | Release strategic sealed granary reserves; mandate legume planting; ration non-staple brewing. |
| **Epidemic Outbreak** | Clustered fever admissions in municipal clinic ledgers; rising infant mortality. | $> 50\%$ increase in weekly fever cases. | Activate drinking water chlorination; enforce quarantine wards; dispatch mobile sanitation teams. |
| **Material Chokepoint** | Maritime freight insurance spikes; tin/timber price inflation $> 100\%$. | Raw metal inventory drops below 6 months of workshop consumption. | Ration strategic metals; transition to local substitute alloys; deploy naval escorts. |
| **Institutional Corruption** | Divergence between physical warehouse inventories and scribal audit ledgers. | Discrepancy exceeds $2.0\%$ of total audited stock. | Immediate independent cross-audit; rotate warehouse managers; publish transparent ledger reconciliations. |
| **Industrial Safety Drift** | Unreported near-misses; operators bypassing pressure relief valves or guards. | Any undocumented repair on a high-pressure or high-voltage boundary. | Immediate shutdown of equipment; mandatory safety retraining; legal enforcement of safety codes. |
