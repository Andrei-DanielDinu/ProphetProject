# Risk Management: Failure Modes and System Vulnerabilities

> "To engineer a resilient civilization, you must anticipate every way it can break."

---

## 1. Technological Failure Modes

1. **Boiler Explosions (Phase 4)**: Caused by scale accumulation, low water levels, or overpressure; mitigated by mandatory dual safety valves, water gauge glasses, and periodic hydrostatic testing.
2. **Semiconductor Wafer Contamination (Phase 6)**: Airborne particulates or organic vapors killing die yields; mitigated by laminar flow HEPA/ULPA filtration, positive pressure cleanrooms, and automated wafer transport pods.
3. **Nuclear Core Uncovery / LOCA (Phase 6)**: Loss of primary coolant leading to fuel overheating; mitigated by passive core flooding accumulators, negative void coefficients, and multi-barrier containment.
4. **AI Alignment Loss (Phase 6)**: Autonomous decision-making models optimizing for perverse unintended proxy objectives; mitigated by isolated air-gaps, mathematical formal verification, and analog hardwired kill-switches.

---

## 2. Social and Ecological Failure Modes

- **Elite Monopolization**: Technology captured by a small ruling caste to oppress citizens; mitigated by distributed education, public open standards, and legal rights of exit.
- **Resource Depletion**: Deforestation or groundwater over-extraction collapsing productive capacity; mitigated by strict ecological resource accounting, reforestation, and recycling mandates.
