# Risk Management: Contingency Plans and Graceful Degradation

> "True resilience is not preventing all failures. True resilience is the ability to fail gracefully, preserve core capability, and rebuild rapidly."

---

## 1. The Graceful Degradation Hierarchy

When catastrophic disruptions (wars, volcanic winters, global network failures) strike advanced technological infrastructure, systems must step down gracefully without total collapse:

```
[ Tier 6: High-Performance AI, EUV Fabs, Spacecraft ]
               |
               v (Graceful Step-Down)
[ Tier 5: Polyphase AC Grid, Analog Radio, Petrochemistry ]
               |
               v (Graceful Step-Down)
[ Tier 4: Steam Pumping, Bessemer Steel, Lathes, Telegraphy ]
               |
               v (Graceful Step-Down)
[ Tier 3: Waterwheels, Bloomery Iron, Printing Press, Windmills ]
               |
               v (Graceful Step-Down)
[ Tier 1-2: Sealed Granaries, Basin Irrigation, Bronze Tools, Clean Water ]
```

### Protocol for Technical Preservation
1. **Preserve Master Measurement Standards**: Protect master length, mass, and electrical calibration gauges in subterranean granite vaults.
2. **Offline Knowledge Vaults**: Preserve complete microfiche and stone-etched technical documentation that requires no electrical grid or computer hardware to read.
3. **Local Foundry Autonomy**: Ensure every regional town maintains at least one manual blacksmith forge, foundry cupola, and water-powered mill capable of maintaining basic tools independently of national supply chains.
