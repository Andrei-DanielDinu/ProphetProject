## 2. The Bottleneck Theorem

If intellectual discovery is compressed to zero, what limits speed?
The Prophet's timeline is bounded solely by three physical constraints:

1. **Extraction and Transit Time**: The days required for donkey caravans or Nile barges to travel to Wadi Dara, extract 50 tons of malachite, and float it to Memphis.
2. **Thermal and Reaction Kinetics**: The hours required for charcoal to burn, limestone to calcine, glass to anneal, and crops to grow.
3. **Tool Bootstrapping Tiers (The Ladder of Precision)**:
   - You must have stone chisels to quarry limestone for kilns.
   - You must have kilns to smelt copper.
   - You must have copper and bronze tools to cut hardwood and carve boring mills.
   - You must have boring mills to make iron cylinders for steam engines.
   - You must have steam engines to drive rolling mills and wire drawing benches.
   - You must have wire drawing to build dynamos and transformers.

Because each tier requires the physical tools of the previous tier, development progresses in distinct, explosive **bootstrap waves**, each lasting weeks or months rather than centuries.
