# References and Foundational Research Domains

The Prophet Project synthesizes empirical knowledge across several major domains of human inquiry:

## 1. Archaeological Sciences and Archaeometallurgy
- Radiocarbon dating and stratigraphy in Early Dynastic Egyptian contexts
- Slag analysis, tuyere morphology, and furnace wall vitrification
- Petrographic and chemical composition of ancient ceramics and mortars
- Trace element analysis and lead isotope sourcing for ancient copper and tin

## 2. Classical Kinematics and Machine Dynamics
- Theoretical mechanics of simple machines (levers, wedges, screws, pulleys)
- Hydrodynamics of open flumes, undershot, and overshot waterwheels
- Surface contact friction, wear mechanics, and hydrodynamic lubrication
- Kinematic linkages, cam profiles, and rotational power transmission

## 3. Chemical Thermodynamics and Process Metallurgy
- Boudouard reaction equilibria and iron oxide reduction kinetics
- Phase diagrams of binary copper-tin and iron-carbon alloys
- Heterogeneous gas-solid catalysis in high-pressure reaction vessels
- Industrial mass balances and closed-loop reagent recovery processes

## 4. Electromagnetism and Solid-State Physics
- Maxwellian field equations and electromagnetic induction in rotary machines
- Dielectric breakdown thresholds and transmission line loss modeling
- Semiconductor bandgap physics, doping profiles, and MOS field-effect behavior
- Cryogenic superconductivity, Meissner effects, and superfluid cooling regimes

## 5. Aerothermodynamics and Spaceflight Engineering
- Supersonic compressible flow through convergent-divergent nozzles
- Tsiolkovsky mass fraction dynamics and multi-stage delta-v budgeting
- Regenerative cooling channel heat flux transfer calculations
- Closed-loop environmental life support recycling stoichiometry
