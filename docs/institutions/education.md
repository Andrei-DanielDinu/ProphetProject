# Institutions: Education Systems and Apprentice Examination

> "Education is the transmission of civilizational capability across biological generations."

---

## 1. Multi-Tier Educational Curriculum

1. **Primary Civic Literacy and Numeracy (Ages 7–12)**: Reading, writing, base-10 mathematics, geometry, basic sanitary hygiene, and empirical observation.
2. **Polytechnic Apprenticeship (Ages 13–18)**: Practical workshop immersion in surveying, metallurgy, carpentry, pottery, or pharmacology alongside certified masters.
3. **Master Guild Academies (Ages 19+)**: Advanced materials engineering, machine design, chemical process control, epidemiology, and institutional administration.

---

## 2. Practical Examination and Peer Review

Certification is never awarded through political patronage or family inheritance:
- **The Blind Masterpiece**: Every prospective master craftsman must submit an independently manufactured masterpiece (e.g., a balanced equal-arm scale, a bored cylinder, an optical lens) tested against objective master calibration standards.
- **The Replication Exam**: A prospective instructor must successfully guide a novice student to perform a complex technical process without intervening physically.
