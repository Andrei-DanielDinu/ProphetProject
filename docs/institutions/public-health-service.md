# Institutions: The Public Health Service and Epidemic Control

> "The first duty of medicine is prevention; the second is care; the third is empirical study."

---

## 1. Municipal Health Officers

Every municipality and major workshop compound appoints a dedicated Public Health Officer empowered to:
- Inspect public drinking water intakes, cisterns, and filtration beds weekly.
- Verify that latrines and animal waste pits are maintained at legal distances from water tables.
- Mandate quarantine protocols during contagious respiratory or enteric outbreaks.

---

## 2. Hospital and Clinical Protocol

1. **Isolation Wards**: Separate patients with acute contagious fevers, diarrhea, or coughing into well-ventilated pavilions isolated from surgical and obstetric wards.
2. **Standardized Clinical Ledgers**: Record patient symptoms, pulse rates, treatments administered, and outcomes in standardized medical notebooks, enabling statistical analysis of treatment efficacy.
3. **No Secret Remedies**: All pharmaceutical formulations must be recorded with exact botanical/chemical dosages and published for peer medical review.
