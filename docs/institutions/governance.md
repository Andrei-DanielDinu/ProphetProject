# Institutions: Governance and Succession Architecture

> "Institutions are the memory of a civilization. A brilliant individual leaves only memories; a robust institution leaves capable successors."

---

## 1. Principles of Civilizational Continuity

1. **Decentralized Decision Rights**: Authority must not concentrate in a single palace or ministry. Regional councils (assemblies of master craftsmen, agronomists, physicians, and civic magistrates) govern day-to-day operations.
2. **Open-Source Standard Specifications**: All formulas, mechanical blueprints, chemical recipes, and legal judgments must be recorded in publicly readable archives rather than kept as esoteric trade secrets.
3. **Institutional Stress Testing**: Periodically test the succession system by removing key leaders for extended sabbaticals to verify that operations, training, and auditing continue without interruption.
