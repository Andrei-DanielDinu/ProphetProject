# Institutions: Technical Workshops and Autonomous Guilds

> "A workshop is where physical theory meets material reality. It is the engine room of civilization."

---

## 1. Structure of the Civic Workshop

Every specialized civic workshop incorporates three physically segregated zones:

1. **Production Bay**: High-throughput fabrication, casting, machining, or chemical digestion.
2. **Calibration and Metrology Chamber**: A climate-moderated, dust-controlled room housing master measurement standards, precision balances, optical comparators, and inspection gauges.
3. **Apprentice Classroom and Archive**: A quiet room with benches, blackboards, drawing tables, and duplicate technical manuals where students copy blueprints and study physical theory.

---

## 2. Guild Responsibilities and Quality Marks

- **Standard Quality Stamping**: Every batch of bronze ingots, forged steel chisels, glass lenses, or chemical reagents must be stamped with the guild's mark and the artisan's personal seal.
- **Product Liability**: Defective tools that fracture under standard operating loads are investigated by the guild's inspection board. Systematic defects require recalling the batch and retraining the workshop crew.
