# Institutions: Standards, Calibration, and Inspection Bureaus

> "Without shared standards of length, weight, time, and purity, trade is friction and engineering is impossible."

---

## 1. The Central Bureau of Standards

An autonomous institution charged with maintaining master physical references:

1. **Master Prototypes**: Stored in temperature-stable subterranean granite vaults:
   - Primary Length Bar (Platinum-Iridium or polished quartz).
   - Primary Mass Standard (polished cylindrical kilogram).
   - Master Optical Flat Plates (Whitworth 3-plate set).
2. **Periodic Re-Calibration Mandate**: Every regional workshop and commercial market must bring its working weights, measures, and dial gauges to the local inspection office every 12 months for verification against secondary standards.
3. **Traceability Chain**: Every gauge in an operational factory must trace its calibration through a verified chain of comparisons back to the master national standard.
