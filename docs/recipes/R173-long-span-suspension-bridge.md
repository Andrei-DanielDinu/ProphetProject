╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 1: DEEP-WATER PNEUMATIC CAISSON FOUNDATIONS               ║
║    1a. Construct heavy steel-shod cutting shoes on riverbank.    ║
║    1b. Float out and sink caisson over designated tower pier     ║
║        location in 30 meters of water.                           ║
║    1c. Build up reinforced concrete perimeter walls [→ R161] as  ║
║        caisson sinks by its own weight under excavation.         ║
║    1d. Pressurized air lock: Pump compressed air into lower      ║
║        working chamber to counter hydrostatic water head. Miners ║

║        (45 meters below water surface).                          ║
║    1e. Bedrock plug: Clean solid bedrock; fill working chamber   ║

║        foundation piers.                                         ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 2: STRUCTURAL STEEL TOWERS & ANCHORAGE BLOCKS             ║

║        anchored to caisson piers: Two vertical tapered legs      ║
║        cross-braced with horizontal strut portals.               ║
║    2b. Cast massive gravity anchorages on each shore: Monolithic ║
║        concrete blocks (60,000 tonnes each) embedded in native   ║
║        rock strata to resist 40,000 tonnes horizontal cable pull.║
║    2c. Install forged alloy steel eyebar chains and anchorage    ║
║        splay saddles deeply inside the anchor blocks.            ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 3: AERIAL CABLE SPINNING (PARALLEL-WIRE TECHNOLOGY)       ║
║    3a. Pull pilot ropes across river span by boat; hoist over    ║
║        tower tops and tension to form temporary catwalk footbridge║
║    3b. Aerial spinning carriage: Spinning wheel shuttles back    ║
║        and forth across the 2,000 m distance at 5 m/s, pulling   ║
║        loops of 5 mm high-tensile galvanized wire from shore     ║
║        reels to opposite anchorage:                              ║
║        Spins 19,000 individual parallel wires per main cable!    ║
║    3c. Parallel wire orientation: Unlike twisted rope, parallel  ║
║        straight wires achieve 100% full steel modulus of         ║
║        elasticity ($E = 205\text{ GPa}$) with zero cable stretch.║
║    3d. Hydraulic compaction: Squeeze loose wires into a dense    ║
║        circular cylinder (diameter 650 mm) under 150 bar radial  ║
║        hydraulic press jaws; void ratio drops to <18%.           ║
║    3e. Continuous spiral wire wrapping and red-lead sealing paste║

╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣

║    4a. Bolt cast steel cable bands around main cables at 15 m    ║
║        intervals; hang vertical multi-strand wire rope suspenders║
║    4b. Aerodynamic deck profile: Prefabricate closed orthotropic ║
║        steel streamlined box girders (aerodynamic wing profile   ║
║        with side wind fairings): Completely immune to torsional  ║
║        flutter and aeroelastic vortex shedding (eliminates the   ║
║        Tacoma Narrows instability!).                             ║
║    4c. Hoist deck sections with floating derrick cranes; pin     ║
║        to suspenders and weld deck joints continuously [→ R040]. ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
