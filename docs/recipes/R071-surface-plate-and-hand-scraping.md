# R071 — Precision Surface Plate and Three-Plate Hand Scraping

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R071 · PRECISION SURFACE PLATE (THREE-PLATE METHOD)      ║
║  End product: 3 × Cast Iron Master Surface Plates (300 × 300 mm) ║
║  Flatness tolerance: < 2.5 micrometers (0.0001 inch) across face║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHY THIS IS THE DATUM PLANE OF INDUSTRIAL CIVILIZATION          ║
║    You cannot build a precision lathe, micrometer, steam engine, ║
║    dynamo, or vacuum pump without straight lines and flat planes.║
║    How do you make the FIRST truly flat surface when you have    ║
║    no flat surface to copy?                                      ║
║    Answer: Sir Joseph Whitworth's Three-Plate Principle (1840).  ║
║    Two plates rubbed together can fit perfectly if one is convex ║
║    and the other concave (a sphere). But THREE plates (A, B, C)  ║
║    can ONLY fit interchangeably if all three are ABSOLUTELY FLAT.║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║  A) 3 × Heavy ribbed cast iron plates (300 × 300 × 50 mm)        ║
║     [→ R071-Cast from R040/bloomery iron remelted in cupola]     ║
║     Deep honeycomb ribs on underside to prevent structural flex. ║
║     Aged/weathered 3 months outdoors to relieve internal casting ║
║     stress before final scraping.                                ║
║  B) 100g Marking pigment paste (Prussian Blue or Lampblack)      ║
║     - Lampblack soot scraped from oil lamp [→ R010]              ║
║     - Mixed with 50 mL rendered tallow or olive oil [→ R051]     ║
║  C) 1 × Hardened high-carbon steel flat scraper [→ R041 / R047]  ║
║     Tungsten-carbide or 1.2% C tool steel hardened to HV 650+.   ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  THE THREE-PLATE ALGORITHM (Deterministic Convergence to Flat)   ║
║                                                                  ║
║  Label plates: A, B, and C.                                      ║
║                                                                  ║
║  CYCLE 1: MATCHING A AND B                                       ║
║    1a. Smear a microscopic film of lampblack paste onto Plate A. ║
║    1b. Invert Plate B onto Plate A. Rub lightly in figure-8      ║
║        patterns with zero downward force (gravity only).         ║
║    1c. Separate plates: high spots on Plate B pick up dark       ║
║        pigment spots.                                            ║
║    1d. Hold hand scraper at 35° angle. Scrape off the dark       ║
║        spots with forward push strokes (shaving ~2 μm of iron).  ║
║    1e. Repeat until Plate A and B show 50% bearing contact.      ║
║                                                                  ║
║  CYCLE 2: MATCHING B AND C                                       ║
║    2a. Coat Plate B with thin lampblack film.                    ║
║    2b. Rub Plate C on Plate B. Scrape high spots on Plate C.     ║
║    2c. Repeat until B and C match.                               ║
║                                                                  ║
║  CYCLE 3: THE ERROR CORRECTION (A AGAINST C)                     ║
║    3a. Now rub Plate C on Plate A.                               ║
║    3b. If A was convex and B was concave: C became convex.       ║
║        When A (convex) meets C (convex), they will ONLY touch    ║
║        at the absolute center point and rock!                    ║
║    3c. Scrape EQUAL amounts of high spots from BOTH A and C.     ║
║                                                                  ║
║  CYCLE 4: ROTATE AND REPEAT                                      ║
║    Rotate pairing: A-B, then B-C, then C-A, then B-A...          ║
║    With every complete rotation cycle, spherical and saddle      ║
║    errors cancel mathematically.                                 ║
║    After 40 hours of scraping: All three plates converge to an   ║
║    absolute optical plane with 25 bearing spots per square inch. ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  CALIBRATION TEST: Clean both plates with alcohol. Slide Plate A ║
║  horizontally onto Plate B. Atmospheric pressure and molecular   ║
║  contact wring the plates together: you can lift Plate B (25 kg) ║
║  into the air purely by holding Plate A!                         ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
