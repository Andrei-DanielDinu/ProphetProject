# R028 — Synthetic NPK Fertilizers & Granulation

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R028 · SYNTHETIC BALANCED NPK FERTILIZER (15-15-15)      ║
║  End product: 1,000 kg Granulated Compound NPK Fertilizer        ║
║  Composition: 15% N (Nitrogen), 15% P₂O₅ (Phosphate), 15% K₂O (Potash)║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PURPOSE IN RECURSIVE CHAIN                                      ║
║    The massive agricultural yield multiplier prerequisite for:   ║
║    • Multiplying crop caloric yields 400% across the Nile valley ║
║    • Sustaining millions of industrial and scientific workers    ║
║    • Eradicating seasonal famine, soil exhaustion, and hunger    ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  RECURSIVE INGREDIENT & TOOL DEPENDENCIES                        ║
║    1. Anhydrous Ammonia (NH₃): 185 kg [→ R055 Haber-Bosch]       ║
║    2. Nitric Acid (HNO₃, 60%): 440 kg [→ Ostwald oxidation R055] ║
║    3. Phosphorite / Phosphate Rock (Ca₃(PO₄)₂): 300 kg [→ R000-F]║
║    4. Sulfuric Acid (H₂SO₄, 93%): 280 kg [→ R053 lead chamber]   ║
║    5. Potassium Chloride (KCl / Sylvite, 60% K₂O): 250 kg [→ R000]║
║    6. Lead-Lined Digestion Reactor: 1 [→ R053, R040]             ║
║    7. Rotary Drum Granulator & Counter-Current Drier: 1 [→ R072] ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 1: NITROGEN COMPONENT (AMMONIUM NITRATE SOLUTION)         ║
║    1a. React anhydrous ammonia gas with 60% nitric acid:         ║
║        NH₃ + HNO₃ ──► NH₄NO₃ (Ammonium Nitrate).                 ║
║        Reaction is intensely exothermic; steam generated is used ║
║        to concentrate solution to 95% molten salt liquor at 140°C║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 2: PHOSPHORUS COMPONENT (WET-PROCESS PHOSPHORIC ACID)     ║
║    2a. Pulverize phosphate rock to <200 mesh.                    ║
║    2b. Digest in sulfuric acid and recycled weak phosphoric acid:║
║        Ca₃(PO₄)₂ + 3 H₂SO₄ + 6 H₂O ──►                           ║
║        2 H₃PO₄ (Phosphoric Acid) + 3 CaSO₄·2H₂O↓ (Gypsum).       ║
║    2c. Filter out crystallized gypsum cake on horizontal belt    ║
║        filter; concentrate phosphoric acid to 54% P₂O₅.          ║
║    2d. Neutralize phosphoric acid with ammonia gas:              ║
║        H₃PO₄ + NH₃ ──► NH₄H₂PO₄ (Monoammonium Phosphate / MAP).  ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 3: POTASSIUM INCORPORATION & DRUM GRANULATION             ║
║    3a. Grind mineral sylvite (KCl) to fine powder.               ║
║    3b. Feed into inclined rotary granulator drum:                ║
║        • Solid feed: Ground KCl (250 kg) + recycled fines (400 kg║
║        • Liquid feed: Hot ammonium nitrate liquor + molten MAP   ║
║    3c. Internal tumbling motion coats solid crystal nuclei with  ║
║        molten liquid slurry, forming spherical granules via      ║
║        capillary snowball aggregation at 85°C.                   ║
║    3d. Discharge into rotary drum drier heated with burner gas   ║
║        to dry granules to <1.0% moisture.                        ║
║    3e. Screen through double-deck vibrating screens: Select      ║
║        2.0 mm to 4.0 mm granules; crush oversize and recycle.    ║
║    3f. Apply 0.2 wt% amine anti-caking oil coating to prevent    ║
║        granules from caking during bulk warehouse storage.       ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  QUALITY CONTROL METRICS                                         ║
║    • Chemical assay: Total N = 15.0 ± 0.5%, Available P₂O₅ =     ║
║      15.0 ± 0.5%, Soluble K₂O = 15.0 ± 0.5%.                     ║
║    • Granule crush strength: >25 N per granule (non-friable).    ║
║    • Water solubility: >95% soluble in ambient water.            ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
