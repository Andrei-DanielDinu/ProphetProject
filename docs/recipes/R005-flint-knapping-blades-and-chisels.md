# R005 — Flint Knapping: Blades, Burins, and Scrapers

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R005 · FLINT KNAPPING — BLADES, BURINS & DRILLS          ║
║  End product: Razor-sharp flint microblades, burins, and awls    ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHY THIS MATTERS                                                ║
║    A freshly knapped flint edge has an edge radius of ~30 Ångström║
║    (3 nanometers) — sharper than any surgical steel scalpel.     ║
║    It cuts leather, carves wood, scores stone, shears cordage,   ║
║    and bores holes in stone before any metal exists.             ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║  A) 1 × High-quality flint or chert nodule (1–3 kg)              ║
║     [→ R000-A] ■ TERMINAL. Conchoidal fracture, uniform core.    ║
║  B) 1 × Leather palm pad (thick cattle hide) [→ R000-K]         ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  TOOLS                                                           ║
║    • Hard hammerstone: 300g round quartzite pebble (R001)        ║
║    • Soft hammer: Antler billet (thick base of deer antler)      ║
║    • Pressure flaker: Antler tine or copper rod (later)          ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PROCESS                                                         ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 1 — PREPARING THE STRIKING PLATFORM                        ║
║    1a. Strike the top of the flint nodule with the quartzite     ║
║        hammerstone to cleave off a flat cap ("striking platform").║
║    1b. The angle between platform and core face must be <85°     ║
║        for conchoidal shockwaves to detach long blades.          ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 2 — BLADE CORE DETACHMENT (Direct Percussion)              ║
║    2a. Rest flint core on leather pad placed over your knee.     ║
║    2b. Place antler billet 2 mm in from the edge of the platform.║
║    2c. Strike billet sharply with hammerstone.                   ║
║    2d. Shockwave propagates down the core ridge, peeling off a   ║
║        straight, razor-sharp flint blade 10–15 cm long × 2 cm wide.║
║    2e. Repeat around perimeter: one 2 kg core yields 30+ blades. ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 3 — RETOUCHING BURINS AND DRILLS (Pressure Flaking)        ║
║    3a. To make a chisel/burin: snap blade tip at 90° to form a   ║
║        thick, robust cutting corner.                             ║
║    3b. To make a drill bit: press pointed antler tip firmly into ║
║        blade edges, popping off tiny micro-flakes to taper point.║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  OUTPUT: 10 surgical blades, 5 drill awls per 1 hour knapping.   ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
