# R051 — Rendered Tallow (Animal Fat Purification)

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R051 · PURIFIED RENDERED TALLOW                         ║
║  End product: 7 kg pure, water-free animal tallow                ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PURPOSE IN RECURSIVE CHAIN                                      ║
║    Purified saturated triglycerides required for:                ║
║    • Hard soap manufacturing [→ R050]                            ║
║    • Heavy mechanical bearing lubrication [→ Manual 04]          ║
║    • Rawhide waterproofing & conditioning [→ R001 / R044]        ║
║    • Pitch lap optical polishing compound [→ R061]               ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║  A) 10 kg Raw suet / cattle pelvic & kidney fat [→ R000-K]       ║
║  B) 4 L Fresh water [→ R000-H]                                   ║
║  C) 50g Salt (NaCl) [→ R000-L] (assists protein coagulation)     ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  TOOLS: 15 L Fired clay pot (R020), stirring paddle, woven cloth ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PROCESS                                                         ║
║  1. Finely dice suet with flint/steel blade to 1 cm cubes.       ║
║  2. Simmer gently in 4 L water at 80–85°C for 2.5 hours.         ║
║  3. Fat cells burst; liquid tallow floats; cellular membranes    ║
║     shrivel into cracklings.                                     ║
║  4. Strain liquid through linen/halfa mesh into clean pot.       ║
║  5. Cool for 8 hours: tallow hardens into a pure white cake      ║
║     floating above separated wastewater and gelatin.             ║
║  6. Lift cake, scrape off bottom grey layer, remelt to 75°C to   ║
║     flash off residual water. Store in sealed ceramic jars.      ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  OUTPUT: 7.0 kg tallow. Melting point: 42–45°C. Acid value: <2.  ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
