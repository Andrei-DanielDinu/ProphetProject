║  AERODYNAMIC LIFT PHYSICS                                        ║
║    A cambered wing airfoil deflects airflow downward; by Newton's║
║    third law, the air exerts an equal upward force (Lift):       ║
║    L = ½ × ρ × v² × S × C_L                                      ║
║    Where:                                                        ║
║      ρ = air density (1.225 kg/m³ at sea level)                  ║
║      v = airspeed in m/s                                         ║
║      S = wing planform area (20 m²)                              ║
║      C_L = lift coefficient determined by airfoil angle of attack║
║    At 50 m/s (180 km/h) with C_L = 0.5:                          ║
║    Lift = 0.5 × 1.225 × (50)² × 20 × 0.5 = 15,312 N (1.5 tonnes!)║
║    Weight of aircraft is 1,200 kg: aircraft climbs effortlessly! ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  METALLURGY: DURALUMIN 2024 ALLOY [→ R041 / R030]                ║
║    Pure aluminum is too weak for flight (tensile 70 MPa).        ║
║    Duralumin composition: 93.5% Al + 4.4% Cu + 1.5% Mg + 0.6% Mn.║
║    Precipitation Heat Treatment (Alfred Wilm, 1906):             ║
║    1. Heat alloy to 500°C (copper dissolves into solid solution).║
║    2. Quench immediately in cold water: frozen soft solution.    ║
║    3. Age at room temperature for 4 days: sub-microscopic        ║
║       copper-aluminum (CuAl₂) intermetallic crystal needles      ║
║       precipitate throughout the metal matrix, pinning slip      ║
║       planes and skyrocketing tensile strength to 450 MPa!       ║
║    (Stronger than structural steel at one-third the weight!).    ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  AIRFRAME CONSTRUCTION (STRESSED-SKIN MONOCOQUE)                ║
║                                                                  ║
║  1. WING STRUCTURE                                               ║
║     • Twin Duralumin I-beam main spars running root to tip.      ║
║     • Hydro-formed 2024-T3 wing ribs spaced every 30 cm          ║
║       (NACA 2412 asymmetric cambered airfoil section).           ║
║     • Skin: 0.8 mm Duralumin sheet flush-riveted to ribs and     ║
║       spars: skin carries primary torsional shear loads!         ║
║                                                                  ║
║  2. THREE-AXIS FLIGHT CONTROL SURFACES                           ║
║     • Ailerons (on wing tips, operated differential by yoke):    ║
║       Roll control around longitudinal axis.                     ║
║     • Elevator (on horizontal tailplane, operated fore-aft):     ║
║       Pitch control around lateral axis (climbs/dives).          ║
║     • Rudder (on vertical tail fin, operated by foot pedals):    ║
║       Yaw control around vertical axis.                          ║
║     • Connected via 3 mm flexible aircraft-grade steel cable     ║
║       pulleys (R084) to pilot controls.                          ║
║                                                                  ║
║  3. VARIABLE-PITCH PROPELLER                                     ║
║     • Forged duralumin twin-blade propeller (2.1 m diameter).    ║
║     • Twist pitch decreases toward tip to maintain uniform       ║
║       angle of attack as tangential tip speed approaches Mach 0.7║
║     • Powered by 150 kW aero engine (R240 scaled).               ║
