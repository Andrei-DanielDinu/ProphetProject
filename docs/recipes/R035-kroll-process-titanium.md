# R035 — Titanium Extraction via the Kroll Process

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R035 · TITANIUM METAL SPONGE (KROLL PROCESS)             ║
║  End product: 50 kg Pure Titanium Metal Sponge (Ti, >99.5%)      ║
║  Density: 4.51 g/cm³, Melting point: 1,668°C, High Strength/Wt   ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PURPOSE IN RECURSIVE CHAIN                                      ║
║    The ultra-strength, lightweight, corrosion-proof metal for:   ║
║    • Gas turbine jet engine compressor disks and blades [→ R246] ║
║    • High-pressure liquid oxygen and hydrogen rocket tanks [→ R111]║
║    • Deep-sea marine hulls, chemical autoclaves, surgical implants║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  RECURSIVE INGREDIENT & TOOL DEPENDENCIES                        ║
║    1. Rutile Mineral Concentrate (TiO₂, >95%): 90 kg [→ R000-F]  ║
║    2. Calcined Petroleum Coke / Pure Charcoal: 25 kg [→ R025]    ║
║    3. Chlorine Gas (Cl₂ anhydrous): 160 kg [→ R052 brine chlor]  ║
║    4. Magnesium Metal Ingots (Mg, 99.8%): 55 kg                  ║
║       [→ Electrolysis of molten MgCl₂]                           ║
║    5. Argon Gas Shield (Ar, >99.99%): 15 m³ [→ R110 cryo air]    ║
║    6. Fluidized Bed Chlorinator & Condensers: 1 [→ R040, R036]   ║
║    7. Stainless Steel Vacuum Reduction Retort: 1 [→ R036, R092]  ║
║    8. Vacuum Arc Remelting (VAR) Furnace: 1 [→ R080, R092]       ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 1: CARBOCHLORINATION (TiO₂ TO TITANIUM TETRACHLORIDE)     ║
║    1a. Pelletize 90 kg rutile TiO₂ with 25 kg calcined coke and  ║
║        pitch binder. Dry at 300°C.                               ║
║    1b. Feed pellets into fluidized bed chlorinator lined with    ║
║        refractory silica at 950°C. Pass dry Cl₂ gas upward:      ║
║        TiO₂ + 2 C + 2 Cl₂ ──► TiCl₄↑ + 2 CO↑.                    ║
║    1c. Condense crude liquid TiCl₄ (boiling point 136.4°C).      ║
║    1d. Fractional distillation through packed 316L column:       ║
║        Separate volatile VOCl₃, FeCl₃, and SiCl₄ impurities.     ║
║        Collect pure, water-white TiCl₄ liquid (210 kg).          ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 2: MAGNESIOTHERMIC REDUCTION (KROLL RETORT)               ║
║    2a. Charge 55 kg solid magnesium ingots into sealed 316L      ║
║        stainless steel retort. Evacuate to <10⁻² torr [→ R092],   ║
║        then backfill with ultra-pure argon gas to 1.1 bar.        ║
║    2b. Heat retort in resistance furnace to 850°C (Mg melts at   ║
║        650°C).                                                   ║
║    2c. Meter pure liquid TiCl₄ into the molten Mg bath:          ║
║        TiCl₄ + 2 Mg ──► Ti (solid sponge) + 2 MgCl₂ (molten).    ║
║        Reaction is strongly exothermic; control feed rate to     ║
║        maintain temperature strictly between 850°C and 900°C.    ║
║    2d. Periodically tap liquid MgCl₂ from the retort bottom      ║
║        (recycle MgCl₂ to electrolysis cells to recover Mg + Cl₂).║
║    2e. After 30 hours, cool retort under continuous argon flow.  ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 3: VACUUM DISTILLATION & CONSOLIDATION                    ║
║    3a. Heat retort to 950°C under vacuum (<0.1 Pa) to evaporate  ║
║        residual entrained Mg and MgCl₂ from the porous sponge.   ║
║    3b. Bore out 50 kg porous titanium sponge cake.               ║
║    3c. Press sponge into consumable electrode and melt in Vacuum ║
║        Arc Remelting (VAR) furnace into solid homogeneous ingot. ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  QUALITY CONTROL METRICS                                         ║
║    • Oxygen content: <0.15% wt (excess oxygen embrittles metal). ║
║    • Nitrogen content: <0.03% wt.                                ║
║    • Tensile strength (annealed): >450 MPa; Elongation: >22%.    ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
