# R029 — Hermetic Food Canning & Autoclave Retort Sterilization

╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R029 · HERMETIC FOOD CANNING & STEAM PRESSURE RETORT     ║

║  Safety: Complete spore inactivation of *Clostridium botulinum*  ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣

║    The strategic food security and military logistics tool for:  ║
║    • Feeding deep mining expeditions, naval fleets, remote armies║
║    • Long-term storage of agricultural surplus against drought   ║
║    • Safe sterile preservation of meats, legumes, and vegetables ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  RECURSIVE INGREDIENT & TOOL DEPENDENCIES                        ║
║    1. Low-Carbon Deep-Drawing Steel Sheet: 60 kg [→ R040]        ║
║    2. Pure Tin Metal Ingot (Sn, 99.9%): 1.5 kg [→ R032]          ║
║    3. Food-Grade Epoxy-Phenolic Internal Lacquer: 2 kg [→ R163]  ║
║    4. Rubberized Sealing Compound Latex: 500 g [→ R164]          ║
║    5. Sanitary Double-Seaming Can Seamer Machine: 1 [→ R072]     ║
║    6. High-Pressure Steam Autoclave Retort (130°C, 3 bar): 1     ║
║       [→ R070 steam boiler, R040]                                ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 1: ELECTROLYTIC TINPLATE & SANITARY CAN BODY              ║
║    1a. Cold-roll low-carbon steel sheet to 0.22 mm thickness     ║
║        with bright surface finish [→ R072 rolling mill].         ║
║    1b. Electroplate 5.6 g/m² tin metal layer on both surfaces    ║
║        from stannous sulfate bath: Tin provides sacrificial      ║
║        cathodic corrosion barrier.                               ║
║    1c. Roll-coat inside surface with epoxy-phenolic lacquer      ║
║        [→ R163]; bake at 200°C for 10 min to create impervious   ║
║        barrier preventing acid attack from sulfur-bearing foods. ║
║    1d. Form cylinder bodies; weld longitudinal seam with copper  ║
║        wire electrode welding wheel; flange both ends outward.   ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 2: FILLING, VACUUM EXHAUST & DOUBLE SEAMING               ║
║    2a. Fill cans with prepared food (meat stew, beans, fish) at  ║
║        minimum 85°C, leaving 8 mm headspace.                     ║
║    2b. Apply round tinplate can end lined with rubberized latex  ║
║        sealant in curled flange.                                 ║
║    2c. Sanitary Double-Seaming (The 5-Fold Hermetic Interlock):  ║
║        • First operation roll: Folds the curl of the can end     ║
║          around the body flange into a loose interlocked hook.   ║
║        • Second operation roll: Exerts massive compressive       ║
║          radial force, squashing the 5 interlocked metal layers  ║
║          tightly against the rubber sealant gasket!              ║
║        Result: 100% hermetic gas-tight seal without any solder!  ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 3: AUTOCLAVE RETORT THERMAL STERILIZATION (12-D PROCESS)  ║
║    3a. Load sealed cans into high-pressure horizontal retort.    ║
║    3b. Steam bleed: Vent exhaust valves with saturated steam     ║
║        [→ R070] for 10 min to eliminate all insulating air pockets.║
║    3c. Pressurize with saturated steam to 1.1 bar gauge (121.1°C ║
║        / 250°F). Apply 1.5 bar overriding compressed air to      ║
║        counter internal pressure and prevent seam deformation.   ║
║    3d. Maintain 121.1°C strictly for 35 minutes:                 ║
║        Achieves an F₀ lethality value >10 minutes: Exceeds the    ║
║        rigorous "12-D process" required to reduce viable spores  ║
║        of *Clostridium botulinum* by 10¹²-fold!                  ║
║    3e. Pressure cooling: Introduce cold water while maintaining  ║
║        overpressure; cool cans to 40°C in 15 minutes. Headspace  ║
║        steam condenses, pulling a strong internal vacuum (can    ║
║        ends snap inward into concave position).                  ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  QUALITY CONTROL METRICS                                         ║
║    • Seam teardown: Seam overlap >1.1 mm, Tightness rating >85%. ║
║    • Hermetic test: Zero bubble leak under 2 bar internal air.   ║
║    • Incubation test: 100 cans incubated at 37°C for 30 days;    ║
║      0 swollen cans, 100% sterile culture assay.                 ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
