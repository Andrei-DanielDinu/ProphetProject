# R038 — Froth Flotation Beneficiation of Sulfide Ores

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R038 · FROTH FLOTATION MINERAL BENEFICIATION             ║
║  End product: 50 tonnes Copper/Lead/Zinc Flotation Concentrate   ║
║  Grade upgrade: 1.0% Cu ore ──► 28.5% Cu concentrate (>92% recovery)║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PURPOSE IN RECURSIVE CHAIN                                      ║
║    The mineral concentration prerequisite that unlocks:          ║
║    • Economic smelting of low-grade porphyry copper ores [→ R030]║
║    • Separation of complex polymetallic Pb-Zn-Cu-Ag sulfide ores ║
║    • Eliminating 90% of inert quartz gangue before smelting      ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  RECURSIVE INGREDIENT & TOOL DEPENDENCIES                        ║
║    1. Low-Grade Copper Sulfide Ore (Chalcopyrite, CuFeS₂): 1,500 t║
║       [→ R000-F, R003 mining atlas]                              ║
║    2. Potassium Amyl Xanthate Collector (PAX): 50 kg             ║
║       [→ Amyl alcohol R054 + CS₂ R053 + KOH R052]                ║
║    3. Pine Oil / Methyl Isobutyl Carbinol (MIBC) Frother: 30 kg  ║
║       [→ Pine wood distillation R025 or petroleum ketone R054]   ║
║    4. Hydrated Quicklime (pH regulator, CaO): 1,500 kg [→ R027]  ║
║    5. Wet Ball Mill & Hydrocyclone Classifier: 1 [→ R037, R072]  ║
║    6. Sub-Aerated Flotation Cells with Impeller & Baffles: 12 pcs║
║       [→ Cast iron R040, rubber lined R164]                      ║
║    7. Rotary Vacuum Disc Concentrate Filter: 1 [→ R036, R092]    ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 1: WET COMMINUTION & LIBERATION SIZE                      ║
║    1a. Primary crushing: Jaw crusher crushes run-of-mine ore to  ║
║        <50 mm. Cone crusher secondary crushes to <10 mm.         ║
║    1b. Wet ball milling: Charge 10 mm crushed ore + water into   ║
║        rotating steel ball mill filled with forged steel balls   ║
║        [→ R040]. Grind to fine slurry pulp (65% solids).         ║
║    1c. Hydrocyclone classification: Slurry is pumped tangentially║
║        into hydrocyclone: Centrifugal force separates coarse     ║
║        particles (recycled back to mill) from fine overflow:     ║
║        80% passing 75 microns (200 mesh): This ensures mineral   ║
║        sulfide grains are physically liberated from quartz rock! ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 2: SURFACE CONDITIONING CHEMISTRY                         ║
║    2a. Slurry pulp enters conditioning tank (35% solids).        ║
║    2b. pH modification: Add milk of lime slurry [→ R027] to raise║
║        pulp pH to 10.5: High pH depresses iron pyrite ($FeS_2$), ║
║        preventing pyrite from floating.                          ║
║    2c. Collector dosing: Add 35 g/tonne Potassium Amyl Xanthate  ║
║        ($C_5H_{11}OCSS^- K^+$): The polar xanthate dithiocarbonate║
║        head chemically bonds to copper atoms on the chalcopyrite ║
║        crystal surfaces; the non-polar hydrocarbon tail points   ║
║        outward, turning the mineral surface intensely hydrophobic║
║        (water-repellent)!                                        ║
║    2d. Frother dosing: Add 15 g/tonne pine oil: Lowers water     ║
║        surface tension, generating stable air bubbles that resist║
║        premature bursting.                                       ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 3: FROTH FLOTATION & FILTRATION                           ║
║    3a. Pulp flows through bank of mechanical flotation cells.    ║
║    3b. High-speed rotor impeller sucks in compressed air; shears ║
║        it into billions of fine 1.0 mm air bubbles rising upward.║
║    3c. Bubble attachment: Hydrophobic xanthate-coated chalcopyrite║
║        particles collide with rising air bubbles, attach to the  ║
║        gas-liquid interface, and float to the surface!           ║
║        Hydrophilic silica/quartz rock remains in the water pulp  ║
║        and sinks to the bottom as waste tailings.                ║
║    3d. Froth skimming: Paddle sweeps thick mineralized copper    ║
║        froth (loaded with shiny metallic gold-colored sulfide    ║
║        crystals) over the cell lip into concentrate launders.    ║
║    3e. Dewatering: Flocculate concentrate; filter on rotary disc ║
║        vacuum filter [→ R092] to produce dry filter cake (9% H₂O)║
║        ready for direct flash smelting [→ R030]!                 ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  QUALITY CONTROL METRICS                                         ║
║    • Concentrate grade: 28.0% to 30.5% Cu by weight.             ║
║    • Copper recovery: $>92.5\%$ of all copper recovered from ore.║
║    • Tailings copper loss: $<0.08\%$ Cu remaining in waste.      ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
