║  FOUR FUNDAMENTAL PROSPECTING LAWS                               ║
║                                                                  ║
║  LAW 1: THE LAW OF GOSSAN ("THE IRON HAT")                       ║
║    Primary sulfide veins (chalcopyrite CuFeS₂, pyrite FeS₂)       ║
║    weather under atmospheric oxygen and rainwater:               ║
║    Sulfur oxidizes to sulfuric acid, which leaches copper down.  ║
║    Iron oxidizes to insoluble ferric hydroxides, leaving a       ║
║    porous, cellular, rusty sponge of dark red-brown-yellow       ║
║    rock cap called a GOSSAN.                                     ║
║    RULE: Wherever you see a dark red, bubbly, sponge-like outcrop║
║    standing out on a hillside, DIG BENEATH IT. At depth (5–15 m) ║
║    lies the secondary enrichment zone (rich copper sulfides).    ║
║                                                                  ║
║  LAW 2: THE LAW OF STRATIGRAPHIC TRACING (ALLUVIAL FLOAT)        ║
║    Heavy minerals erode from mountain veins and wash down wadis. ║
║    Method:                                                       ║
║    1. Walk the mouth of a dry desert canyon (wadi).              ║
║    2. Inspect gravels for "float" (green malachite pebbles,      ║
║       heavy dark cassiterite, black hematite).                   ║
║    3. If float is found, walk UPSTREAM.                          ║
║    4. At every wadi fork, check both branches. Take the branch   ║
║       that contains float. Discard the barren branch.            ║
║    5. When float suddenly ceases: STOP. The vein outcrop is on   ║
║       the hill slope directly adjacent. Climb and locate source. ║
║                                                                  ║
║  LAW 3: HYDROTHERMAL QUARTZ VEIN INTRUSIONS                      ║
║    Metamorphic basement rocks (schists, diorites) fractured by   ║
║    ancient tectonic activity filled with mineral-bearing hot water║
║    solutions. They appear as brilliant white quartz "walls" or   ║
║    veins cutting discordantly across dark surrounding rocks.     ║
║    Look for: green stains (copper), golden flakes (pyrite/gold), ║
║    or heavy black crystal clusters (wolframite/cassiterite).     ║
║                                                                  ║
║  LAW 4: DENSITY AND STREAK DISCRIMINATION                        ║
║    Carry an unglazed white porcelain/limestone tile and a knife. ║
║    Streak test separates fools from kings in 2 seconds:          ║
║    - Hematite: Blood-red / dark brown streak.                    ║
║    - Magnetite: Iron-black streak, magnetic.                     ║
║    - Malachite: Pale green streak; fizzes with acetic acid.      ║
║    - Pyrite (fool's gold): Greenish-black streak, brittle.       ║
║    - Native Gold: Golden-yellow streak, malleable (dents).       ║
║    - Cassiterite: Pale grey-brown streak, density ~7.0 (huge).   ║
