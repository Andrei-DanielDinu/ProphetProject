║  PAVING PROCESS (Hot-Mix Asphalt Method)                         ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 1 — SUBGRADE & MACADAM PREPARATION                         ║
║    1a. Grade roadbed with 2% crown slope (sheds flash rains).    ║
║    1b. Compact native soil with sheep-foot roller.               ║
║    1c. Spread 20 cm crushed stone (30–50 mm angular rocks).      ║
║        Roll dry: rocks lock into rigid self-supporting matrix    ║
║        (John McAdam's principle of interlocking angular stones). ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 2 — HOT-MIX PUGMILL BATCHING                               ║
║    2a. Dry stone and sand aggregate in rotary drum at 160°C      ║
║        (removes all moisture so bitumen sticks permanently).     ║
║    2b. Heat bitumen liquid to 160°C in insulated kettle.         ║
║    2c. Discharge into twin-shaft paddle pugmill mixer:           ║
║        Mix for 45 seconds: hot black bitumen coats 100% of       ║
║        aggregate stones in an impermeable viscous film!          ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 3 — SPREADING AND TANDEM COMPACTION                        ║
║    3a. Haul hot mix (150°C) to roadbed in covered dump trucks.   ║
║    3b. Mechanical asphalt paver screed spreads uniform 5 cm mat. ║
║    3c. BREAKDOWN COMPACTION (Initial rolling at 140°C):          ║
║        10-tonne steel tandem roller makes 2 passes: kneads stone ║
║        and locks aggregate structure.                            ║
║    3d. FINISH COMPACTION (Rolling at 100°C):                     ║
║        Multi-wheel pneumatic tire roller seals surface pores:    ║
║        Density reaches 98% maximum theoretical density.          ║
║    3e. Cool to 40°C: open road to 50-tonne heavy industrial haul!║
