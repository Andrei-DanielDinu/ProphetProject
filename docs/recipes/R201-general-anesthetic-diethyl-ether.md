# R201 — General Anesthetic Diethyl Ether

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R201 · MEDICAL GRADE DIETHYL ETHER ((C2H5)2O)            ║
║  End product: 5 Liters of Pure Anesthetic Grade Ether (>99%)     ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHY ANESTHESIA ENABLES ADVANCED SURGERY                         ║
║    Before October 16, 1846 (William Morton), surgery was butcher ║
║    work performed on screaming, thrashing patients pinned down   ║
║    by orderlies. Amputations took 30 seconds; abdominal surgery  ║
║    was impossible due to fatal surgical shock.                   ║
║    Diethyl ether renders the central nervous system completely   ║
║    insensate, abolishing pain reflexes and muscle tone while     ║
║    maintaining autonomous breathing and cardiac output!          ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  CHEMICAL SYNTHESIS (Continuous Etherification)                 ║
║    Two-stage acid-catalyzed dehydration of ethyl alcohol:        ║
║                                                                  ║
║    Stage 1 (Sulfovinic acid intermediate):                       ║
║    C₂H₅OH + H₂SO₄ ──► C₂H₅HSO₄ + H₂O   (at 100°C)                ║
║                                                                  ║
║    Stage 2 (Nucleophilic substitution):                          ║
║    C₂H₅HSO₄ + C₂H₅OH ──► C₂H₅-O-C₂H₅ (Ether↑) + H₂SO₄  (at 140°C)║
║                                                                  ║
║    The sulfuric acid acts as a continuous catalyst! Water is     ║
║    co-distilled, and fresh alcohol is continuously dripped in.   ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║  A) 10 L Pure Anhydrous Ethanol (95%+ C₂H₅OH)                    ║
║     [→ R050: Fractional pot still rectification over quicklime]  ║
║  B) 5 L Concentrated Sulfuric Acid (95% H₂SO₄) [→ R053]          ║
║  C) 500 mL Sodium hydroxide solution (10% NaOH) [→ R052]         ║
║  D) 500g Anhydrous calcium chloride (CaCl₂) drying agent         ║
║     [Made by reacting limestone R000-J with hydrochloric acid]   ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  EQUIPMENT (Lead-Lined Still with Long Water Condenser)          ║
║    • Heavy borosilicate glass retort or lead-lined copper still  ║
║      [→ R060 / R081]. Fitted with submerged alcohol feed tube.   ║
║    • Accurate mercury/alcohol thermometer reading 100°C–160°C.   ║
║    • 2-meter long Liebig water-jacketed condenser tube.          ║
║    • Ice-water cooled collection receiver vessel.                ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PROCESS                                                         ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 1 — INITIAL MIXING                                         ║
║    1a. Pour 3 L ethanol into retort.                             ║
║    1b. Add 3 L concentrated H₂SO₄ SLOWLY with stirring.          ║
║        Solution turns hot (exothermic) and darkens slightly.     ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 2 — THE REACTION DISTILLATION (Strict Temp Window!)        ║
║    2a. Heat retort gently over sand bath to 140°C.               ║
║    2b. ⚠ CRITICAL TEMPERATURE CONTROL:                           ║
║        - Below 130°C: Alcohol distills unreacted.                ║
║        - Between 135°C and 145°C: Pure diethyl ether vaporizes.  ║
║        - Above 150°C: Side-reaction dominates: ethylene gas      ║
║          (C₂H₄) and toxic sulfur dioxide (SO₂) are produced!     ║
║    2c. Maintain strictly at 140°C. Connect alcohol dripper: feed ║
║        fresh ethanol in at the exact rate that ether distills off║
║        (continuous etherification).                              ║
║    2d. Ether vapors (BP 34.6°C) pass through condenser chilled   ║
║        by running ice water: condenses into water-white liquid.  ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 3 — PURIFICATION & NEUTRALIZATION                          ║
║    3a. The raw distillate contains ether, dissolved SO₂, alcohol.║
║    3b. Wash by shaking in separatory funnel with 500 mL of 10%   ║
║        NaOH solution: neutralizes all acid traces. Separate.     ║
║    3c. Wash twice with saturated salt brine (R000-L).            ║
║    3d. Add anhydrous CaCl₂ pellets: absorbs every trace of water.║
║    3e. Final redistillation over water bath at 35°C–36°C:        ║
║        Collect pure medical grade anesthetic ether!              ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  SAFETY & STORAGE: Vapor is heavier than air and EXPLOSIVE!      ║
║  Zero open flames within 10 meters! Store in dark amber bottles  ║
║  sealed with cork/foil to prevent auto-oxidation to peroxides.   ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
