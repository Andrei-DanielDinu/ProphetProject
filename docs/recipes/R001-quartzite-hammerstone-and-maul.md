# R001 — Quartzite Hammerstone and Hafted Mining Maul

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R001 · QUARTZITE HAMMERSTONE AND HAFTED MAUL             ║
║  End product:                                                    ║
║    Type A: 1 × Hand-held Hammerstone (1.0–1.5 kg)                ║
║    Type B: 1 × Grooved Hafted Mining Maul (3.5–4.5 kg)           ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHY YOU MUST MAKE THIS BEFORE ANY METAL WORK                    ║
║    You cannot mine ore without a hammer to strike wedges.        ║
║    You cannot crush ore without a hammer to break nodules.       ║
║    You cannot knap flint tools without a hammerstone.            ║
║    You cannot consolidate a bloom or forge hot iron into a      ║
║    chisel without a hammer.                                      ║
║    The stone hammer is the PRIMORDIAL TOOL OF MANUFACTURE.       ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║                                                                  ║
║  FOR TYPE A (Hand-Held Hammerstone):                             ║
║  A1) 1 × Dense, fine-grained quartzite or basalt river cobble   ║
║      [→ R000-A/R000-B area gravels] ■ TERMINAL                   ║
║      Mass: 1.0–1.5 kg (palm-filling egg/oval shape).             ║
║      Toughness: Must NOT be brittle flint (flint shatters when   ║
║      striking hard rock). Quartzite (metamorphosed sandstone)    ║
║      or basalt has interlocking mineral grains that absorb shock.║
║                                                                  ║
║  FOR TYPE B (Hafted Heavy Mining/Forging Maul):                  ║
║  B1) 1 × Large cylindrical quartzite or olivine-basalt cobble   ║
║      Mass: 3.5–4.5 kg. Length: 18–22 cm, Diameter: 10–12 cm.   ║
║      [■ TERMINAL — Alluvial gravels / wadi beds]                 ║
║                                                                  ║
║  B2) 1 × Forked acacia or tough tamarisk branch for handle      ║
║      [→ R000-I] ■ TERMINAL. Length: 60 cm, Diameter: 3.5–4.0 cm. ║
║      Must be dense, springy hardwood, free of knots or cracks.   ║
║                                                                  ║
║  B3) 2.5 m fresh wet rawhide thong or split cattle gut          ║
║      [→ R000-K] ■ TERMINAL. Width: 1.5 cm, Thickness: 2–3 mm.   ║
║      Rawhide shrinks 15–20% on drying with hundreds of kg force.║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  TOOLS                                                           ║
║    • Hand-held flint chisel or quartz pecking stone [→ R005]     ║
║    • Wet sand abrasive [→ R000-F] for smoothing                  ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  CONDITIONS                                                      ║
║    • Cobble selection test (Crucial!): Drop the candidate cobble ║
║      from waist height (1 meter) onto a flat granite rock.       ║
║      - If it cracks, chips, or sounds dull: REJECT immediately.  ║
║      - If it bounces with a high-pitched ring: EXCELLENT TOUGHNESS.║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  DIAGRAM: HAFTED MAUL (TYPE B)                                   ║
║                                                                  ║
║          Pecked retention groove (1.5 cm deep, 3 cm wide)        ║
║                    │                                             ║
║         ┌──────────▼──────────┐                                  ║
║         │   STONE MAUL HEAD   │                                  ║
║     ════╡   (Quartzite/Basalt)╞═══════════════════════════       ║
║         │       4.0 kg        │     FORKED ACACIA HANDLE        ║
║         └──────────▲──────────┘       (60 cm long)               ║
║                    │                                             ║
║         Wrapped wet rawhide thong (shrinks rock-solid)           ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PROCESS: MAKING THE HAFTED MAUL (TYPE B)                        ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 1 — PECKING THE CIRCUMFERENTIAL GROOVE (3 hours)           ║
║    1a. Take candidate stone cobble B1 (4 kg). Secure it on sand. ║
║    1b. Using a sharp hand-held quartz or flint point (R005), tap ║
║        perpendicularly along the middle perimeter of the stone. ║
║    1c. Work in continuous rotations. Microscopic dust chips off. ║
║    1d. Target: A circumferential waist groove 1.5 cm deep and    ║
║        3 cm wide completely circling the stone.                  ║
║        This groove mechanically prevents the head from slipping   ║
║        out of the handle lashing during heavy impact blows.      ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 2 — PREPARING THE WOODEN HAFT                              ║
║    2a. Cut green acacia branch with a natural symmetrical fork   ║
║        or split a straight 4 cm branch down the center for 25 cm.║
║    2b. Scrape bark off. Shave the inner contact faces flat so    ║
║        they seat snug into the stone's pecked groove.            ║
║    2c. Heat the split fork gently over smoke/embers to bend the  ║
║        arms around the stone without snapping the fibers.        ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 3 — RAWHIDE LASHING                                        ║
║    3a. Soak 2.5 m rawhide thong in water for 30 minutes until    ║
║        completely limp and slimy.                                ║
║    3b. Place stone head into the handle fork/groove.             ║
║    3c. Wrap wet rawhide tightly in a figure-8 pattern:           ║
║        around the stone groove, through the fork junction,       ║
║        down around the handle shaft. Pull each turn with full    ║
║        body weight. Tie off with a secure friction knot.         ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 4 — DRYING AND CURING                                      ║
║    4a. Leave in dry shade for 48 hours. DO NOT put in hot fire.  ║
║    4b. As rawhide loses water, it contracts with immense tension,║
║        welding stone and wood into a single monolithic unit.     ║
║    4c. Coat rawhide with rendered tallow (R051) to waterproof it.║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  TESTING AND VERIFICATION                                        ║
║    • Strike a 20 kg granite boulder 20 consecutive full-power    ║
║      blows.                                                      ║
║    • Verify zero play or wobble between stone head and handle.   ║
║    • Ready for mining rock, crushing ore, or forge work.         ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  SKILLS: Stone pecking (patience), rawhide binding.              ║
║  CYCLE TIME: 4 hours active work, 48 hours curing.              ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
