╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R054 · PETROLEUM FRACTIONAL DISTILLATION                ║
║  End product: Kerosene (RP-1 Rocket Fuel), Gasoline, Diesel,    ║
║               Lubricating Oils, and Asphalt Bitumen              ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PURPOSE IN RECURSIVE CHAIN                                      ║
║    Fractionated hydrocarbons required for:                       ║
║    • Refined Kerosene (RP-1) rocket engine fuel [→ R110]         ║
║    • High-speed internal combustion engine fuel                  ║
║    • Machine tool spindle and bearing lubricant oils             ║
║    • Transformer dielectric oil [→ R082]                         ║
║    • Waterproofing bitumens and chemical synthesis feedstocks    ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║  A) 1,000 L Raw Crude Petroleum                                  ║
║     [→ R000: Gebel el-Zeit surface seeps & shallow drilled wells ║
║     at 27.9°N, 33.3°E along the Gulf of Suez].                   ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  EQUIPMENT: TRAY DISTILLATION COLUMN                             ║
║    • Steel retort boiler (1,200 L capacity) heated by gas/oil.   ║
║    • Vertical fractionation column: 40 cm diameter × 4 m tall.   ║
║      Contains 12 perforated horizontal bubble-cap trays.         ║
║    • Water-cooled condenser coils (copper/lead) at each takeoff. ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  FRACTIONATION CUT TEMPERATURE PROTOCOL                          ║
║                                                                  ║
║  1. LIGHT NAPHTHA / GASOLINE (Cut: 40°C to 150°C)                ║
║     Yield: ~150 L. C5 to C8 hydrocarbons. Highly volatile,       ║
║     flammable solvent and spark-ignition engine fuel.            ║
║                                                                  ║
║  2. KEROSENE / RP-1 ROCKET FUEL FRACTION (Cut: 150°C to 275°C)   ║
║     Yield: ~250 L. C9 to C16 hydrocarbons. Density: 0.81 g/cm³.  ║
║     Purification for rocketry (RP-1 standard):                   ║
║     Wash cut with 98% sulfuric acid (R053) to remove unsaturated ║
║     alkenes and sulfur aromatics (prevents coking inside cooling  ║
║     channels of rocket nozzles). Wash with NaOH lye (R052), then ║
║     water, dry over quicklime. Clean-burning rocket propellant.  ║
║                                                                  ║
║  3. DIESEL / GAS OIL FRACTION (Cut: 275°C to 350°C)              ║
║     Yield: ~200 L. C15 to C22. Heavy compression-ignition fuel.  ║
║                                                                  ║
║  4. LUBRICATING OIL FRACTION (Vacuum distillation above 350°C)   ║
║     Yield: ~150 L. Viscous motor oil (SAE 30 equivalent).        ║
║                                                                  ║
║  5. RESIDUAL BOTTOMS (Bitumen / Asphalt)                         ║
║     Yield: ~200 kg black solid pitch at room temperature.        ║
║     Road paving, battery box sealant, waterproof linings.        ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
