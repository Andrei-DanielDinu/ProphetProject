# R200 — Antibiotic Penicillin (Deep-Tank Submerged Fermentation)

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R200 · PENICILLIN G (BENZYLPENICILLIN SODIUM CRYSTALS)   ║
║  End product: 100 grams sterile crystalline Penicillin G sodium  ║
║  Potency: 1,600 International Units (IU) per milligram           ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHY THIS SINGLE RECIPE RESHAPES HUMAN SURVIVAL                  ║
║    Before antibiotics, a scratched thorn, childbed fever,       ║
║    bacterial pneumonia, or battlefield wound infection was a     ║
║    50% death sentence.                                           ║
║    Penicillin halts bacterial cell wall synthesis by inhibiting  ║
║    DD-transpeptidase in Gram-positive bacteria (Staphylococcus,  ║
║    Streptococcus, Clostridium), causing cell wall lysis and      ║
║    osmotic rupture while having ZERO toxicity on human cells!    ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║  A) High-yield mold strain: Penicillium chrysogenum (NRRL 1951)  ║
║     [Found naturally on moldy cantaloupe / citrus fruit;         ║
║     isolated by serial dilution on agar petri plates].           ║
║  B) 1,000 L Liquid Fermentation Broth:                          ║
║     - 40 kg Corn Steep Liquor (organic nitrogen & amino acids)   ║
║       [Steep dry maize/corn in warm water with SO₂ for 48h]      ║
║     - 30 kg Lactose / Whey sugar (slow-release carbon source)    ║
║     - 5 kg Glucose (initial rapid growth phase)                  ║
║     - 3 kg Sodium nitrate (NaNO₃) or Ammonium sulfate [→ R055]   ║
║     - 1 kg Phenylacetic acid (side-chain precursor for Pen-G)    ║
║     - 0.5 kg Potassium dihydrogen phosphate (KH₂PO₄)             ║
║     - 0.25 kg Magnesium sulfate heptahydrate (MgSO₄·7H₂O)        ║
║     - 5 L Pure animal/vegetable oil defoamer [→ R051]            ║
║     - 950 L Deionized water [→ R000-H / distillation]            ║
║  C) Extraction reagents:                                         ║
║     - 100 L Amyl acetate or Butyl acetate (organic solvent)      ║
║     - 10 L Sulfuric acid (10% H₂SO₄) [→ R053]                    ║
║     - 5 L Sodium hydroxide solution (10% NaOH) [→ R052]          ║
║     - 5 kg Activated decolorizing charcoal powder [→ R025]       ║
║     - Pure sterile ethanol [→ R050-still]                        ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  EQUIPMENT (FERMENTER & STERILIZATION)                           ║
║    • 1,200 L Jacketed stainless steel or lead-free bronze tank   ║
║      [→ R070 / R040]. High-pressure steam jacket for heating.   ║
║    • High-speed turbine agitator (200 RPM) driven by steam/motor.║
║    • Sparger aeration ring: delivers 500 L/min sterile air.      ║
║    • Cotton wool / glass wool air filter (removes all airborne   ║
║      bacteria and competing wild spores).                        ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  FERMENTATION PROTOCOL                                           ║
║                                                                  ║
║  PHASE 1 — STERILIZATION                                         ║
║    1a. Fill tank with 1,000 L broth. Seal all ports.             ║
║    1b. Inject live steam at 1.5 bar (121°C) for 45 minutes:      ║
║        thermal kill of 100% of native wild contaminants.         ║
║    1c. Cool rapidly to 25°C by passing cold water through jacket.║
║                                                                  ║
║  PHASE 2 — INOCULATION AND AEROBIC FERMENTATION                  ║
║    2a. Inoculate with 50 L of pure vegetative P. chrysogenum     ║
║        pre-culture under sterile flame curtain.                  ║
║    2b. Operating parameters (strictly maintained for 120 hours): ║
║        - Temperature: 24.5°C ± 0.5°C.                            ║
║        - pH: Maintained at 6.8–7.2 with automated/metered NaOH.  ║
║        - Aeration: Continuous sterile air (0.5 vvm = 500 L/min). ║
║        - Agitation: 200 RPM (disperses oxygen bubbles).          ║
║    2c. 0–40 hours: Mold mycelium multiplies exponentially.       ║
║    2d. 40–120 hours: Glucose is exhausted; mold enters idiophase║
║        (secondary metabolism), consuming lactose and secreting   ║
║        massive amounts of Penicillin G into the broth!           ║
║                                                                  ║
║  PHASE 3 — DOWNSTREAM RECOVERY & PURIFICATION                    ║
║    3a. Rotary vacuum drum filter: filter off mycelial mold cake. ║
║        Collect crystal-clear liquid beer containing penicillin.  ║
║    3b. Chill beer immediately to 2°C (penicillin degrades fast). ║
║    3c. Acidify beer to pH 2.5 with cold 10% H₂SO₄.               ║
║        At pH 2.5, penicillin becomes un-ionized free acid.       ║
║    3d. Liquid-liquid solvent extraction: Mix with 100 L cold     ║
║        butyl acetate. Penicillin acid transfers into solvent.    ║
║    3e. Separate solvent layer. Back-extract: Add 10 L water +    ║
║        NaOH until pH rises to 7.0: Penicillin turns into water-  ║
║        soluble sodium penicillin salt and returns to water!      ║
║    3f. Decolorize with activated carbon (R025). Filter carbon.   ║
║    3g. Crystallization: Add sterile butanol/ethanol. Pure white  ║
║        crystalline Sodium Penicillin G precipitates out!         ║
║    3h. Vacuum-dry crystals below 40°C. Seal in sterile vials.    ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  DOSAGE & BIOASSAY TEST                                          ║
║    • Kirby-Bauer disc diffusion: 10 μL on agar plate lawned with ║
║      Staphylococcus aureus produces >25 mm clear zone of kill!   ║
║    • Therapeutic dose: 250,000 IU (156 mg) intramuscularly.      ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
