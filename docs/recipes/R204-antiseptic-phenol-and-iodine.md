# R204 — Antiseptic Phenol & Povidone-Iodine

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R204 · SURGICAL ANTISEPTICS (PHENOL & POVIDONE-IODINE)   ║
║  End product: 50 L Antiseptic Solutions (Carbolic Acid & Betadine)║
║  Efficacy: Complete destruction of vegetative bacteria & spores  ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PURPOSE IN RECURSIVE CHAIN                                      ║
║    The infection-prevention prerequisite for:                    ║
║    • Aseptic surgical procedures, wound debridement, childbirth  ║
║    • Sterilization of medical instruments and operating theaters ║
║    • Prevention of hospital-acquired gangrene and septicemia     ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  RECURSIVE INGREDIENT & TOOL DEPENDENCIES                        ║
║    1. Coal Tar Distillate (Middle Oil 170–240°C): 100 L [→ R054] ║
║    2. Sodium Hydroxide Lye (NaOH, 10% solution): 50 L [→ R052]   ║
║    3. Carbon Dioxide Gas (CO₂): 10 kg [→ R027 lime calcination]  ║
║    4. Seaweed Ash / Caliche Nitrate Brine (Iodine): 5 kg [→ R000]║
║    5. Polyvinylpyrrolidone (PVP polymer): 2 kg [→ R054 acetylene]║
║    6. Glass Fractional Distillation Retort & Condenser: 1 [→ R060]║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 1: PHENOL (CARBOLIC ACID) EXTRACTION & SYNTHESIS          ║
║    1a. Distill crude coal tar [→ R054]. Collect middle oil       ║
║        fraction (boiling 170°C to 230°C, rich in cresols & phenol)║
║    1b. Agitate oil with 10% aqueous NaOH solution:               ║
║        C₆H₅OH + NaOH ──► C₆H₅ONa (Sodium phenolate, water-soluble)║
║        + H₂O. Separate lower aqueous layer from neutral oils.    ║
║    1c. Bubble CO₂ gas through aqueous sodium phenolate solution: ║
║        2 C₆H₅ONa + CO₂ + H₂O ──► 2 C₆H₅OH↓ (Crude Phenol) +      ║
║        Na₂CO₃. Crude oily phenol floats to the top.              ║
║    1d. Separate and fractionally distill through glass column:   ║
║        Collect pure crystalline phenol (melting point 40.5°C,    ║
║        boiling point 181.7°C).                                   ║
║    1e. Formulation: Dilute to 2.5% to 5.0% aqueous solution for   ║
║        instrument soak and antiseptic wound cleansing (Listerian ║
║        carbolic spray).                                          ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 2: POVIDONE-IODINE (NON-STINGING BROAD-SPECTRUM IODOPHOR) ║
║    2a. Extract elemental iodine (I₂) from seaweed ash or mineral ║
║        caliche using chlorine water [→ R052] and sulfuric acid:  ║
║        2 I⁻ + Cl₂ ──► I₂↓ (Sublime into deep violet crystals).   ║
║    2b. Synthesize polyvinylpyrrolidone (PVP) by reacting         ║
║        butyrolactone with ammonia, vinylating with acetylene     ║
║        [→ R054], and polymerizing with H₂O₂.                     ║
║    2c. Dissolve 10% wt PVP in purified water. Add 1% wt          ║
║        elemental iodine and 0.5% potassium iodate (KIO₃).        ║
║    2d. The iodine complexes tightly within the coiled PVP        ║
║        polymer matrix: forms a water-soluble brown iodophor.     ║
║    2e. The complex slowly releases free active elemental iodine  ║
║        in microbicidal equilibrium without tissue irritation or  ║
║        chemical burns.                                           ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  QUALITY CONTROL METRICS                                         ║
║    • Phenol purity: Melting point 40.0–41.0°C.                   ║
║    • Available iodine assay: 0.90% to 1.20% w/v (thiosulfate     ║
║      titration).                                                 ║
║    • Microbial kill rate: 100% kill of *Staphylococcus aureus*   ║
║      and *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* within 60 seconds exposure.    ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
