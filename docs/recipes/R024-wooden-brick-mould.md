# R024 — Wooden Brick Mould

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R024 · WOODEN BRICK MOULD (3-cell)                      ║
║  End product: 1 reusable mould for 3 bricks per press,          ║
║  internal cells 36×18×9 cm, lasts 1,000+ uses.                  ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHAT YOU GET                                                    ║
║    A handled wooden frame that shapes 3 mudbricks simultaneously ║
║    when filled with clay mix and lifted off. No fasteners needed ║
║    if properly made — friction-fit joinery.                      ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║                                                                  ║
║  A) 1 plank: 120 cm × 20 cm × 3 cm (base + sides long)         ║
║     [→ R000-I TERM.] Acacia or sycamore. Seasoned (dry).       ║
║                                                                  ║
║  B) 4 planks: 36 cm × 20 cm × 3 cm (end + divider pieces)      ║
║     [→ R000-I TERM.] Same wood. Same grade.                     ║
║                                                                  ║
║  C) 10 × wooden pegs: 8 mm dia × 50 mm long (joinery pins)     ║
║     [→ R000-I TERM.] Hardwood (acacia preferred). Bone dry.     ║
║                                                                  ║
║  D) 1 × handle plank: 50 cm × 6 cm × 3 cm                      ║
║     [→ R000-I TERM.] Attached to top long side.                 ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  TOOLS                                                           ║
║    • Copper saw (later bronze/iron)        [→ R030/R040]        ║
║    • Copper/flint chisel for mortises      [→ R030]             ║
║    • Sandstone for smoothing surfaces      ■ TERMINAL           ║
║    • Rawhide mallet                        [→ R000-K TERM.]     ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  DIMENSIONS AND ASSEMBLY DIAGRAM                                 ║
║                                                                  ║
║  Top view of assembled mould:                                    ║
║                                                                  ║
║  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐    ║
║  │      HANDLE (50cm) on top edge of long side             │    ║
║  ├──────────┬────────────────────┬────────────────────────┤    ║
║  │  CELL 1  │       CELL 2       │        CELL 3          │    ║
║  │ 36×18 cm │      36×18 cm      │        36×18 cm        │    ║
║  │          │                    │                        │    ║
║  └──────────┴────────────────────┴────────────────────────┘    ║
║  ← 18 cm → ← 36 cm (long side) → ← 18 cm → ← 36 cm →        ║
║  Total outer: 120 cm × 20 cm                                    ║
║                                                                  ║
║  Side view:                                                      ║
║    _________  ← top (open face, struck level with straightedge)  ║
║    │       │  ← 9 cm deep walls                                  ║
║    │       │                                                     ║
║    ─────────  ← open bottom (mould placed on flat ground)        ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PROCESS                                                         ║
║    1. Cut long side planks (A) to 120 × 20 × 3 cm.             ║
║       Cut 2 pieces. These form the two long outer walls.         ║
║    2. Cut end and divider planks (B) to 36 × 20 × 3 cm (4 pcs).║
║       2 are end pieces. 2 are internal dividers.                 ║
║    3. Mark mortise positions on long planks:                     ║
║       Mortises at positions 18 cm, 54 cm, 90 cm from each end   ║
║       (for 2 dividers + 2 ends). Mortise: 3 cm wide × 3 cm deep ║
║       × 20 cm tall slot cut into inner face of long plank.      ║
║    4. Chop mortises with copper/flint chisel. Drive out waste    ║
║       with mallet and chisel. Test fit each cross-piece.         ║
║    5. Assemble dry (no glue needed). Pegs driven through         ║
║       pre-drilled holes (8 mm dia, drilled with bow-drill or    ║
║       copper auger) lock the assembly.                           ║
║    6. Attach handle: peg and lash with rawhide to top of one    ║
║       long plank. Handle allows lifting mould cleanly off bricks.║
║    7. Smooth all interior faces with sandstone block until flat. ║
║       Interior smoothness determines brick face quality.         ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  SKILLS: Basic woodworking. 4 hours to build first mould.       ║
║  LIFETIME: 1,000–5,000 uses. Oil with tallow occasionally.      ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
