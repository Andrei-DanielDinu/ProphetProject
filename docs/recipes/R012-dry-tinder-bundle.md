# R012 — Dry Tinder Bundle

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R012 · DRY TINDER BUNDLE                                ║
║  End product: 1 tinder nest, ~10g, ready to receive a coal      ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHAT YOU GET                                                    ║
║    A loose, airy, bird's-nest-shaped bundle that bursts into     ║
║    open flame within 10–30 seconds of receiving a glowing coal. ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS (choose any 2–3 from list below)                   ║
║    A) Dry grass, fine: Nile reed (Phragmites) or esparto grass  ║
║       Strip fine leaves. Dry in sun 3+ days. 5g.   ■ TERMINAL  ║
║    B) Cattail (Typha) seed fluff: collect brown seed heads from  ║
║       Nile margins. 1g. Ignites at 180°C.           ■ TERMINAL  ║
║    C) Dry cedar or juniper bark shreds: peel from dead branches, ║
║       rub between palms to separate fibres. 4g.     ■ TERMINAL  ║
║    D) Dry papyrus pith: inner white spongy tissue of papyrus     ║
║       stem (Cyperus papyrus), pounded thin. 3g.     ■ TERMINAL  ║
║    E) Dry animal dung (cattle/donkey): fine powdery fraction     ║
║       sifted from dry dung heap. 3g.                ■ TERMINAL  ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  CONDITIONS                                                      ║
║    • Moisture content: ZERO. Any moisture = failed ignition.    ║
║    • Test: pinch fibres between fingers. If cool to touch or    ║
║      feel limp: not dry enough. Sun-dry or warm near a fire      ║
║      for 10 minutes before use.                                  ║
║    • Storage: in sealed clay pot or leather pouch.              ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PROCESS                                                         ║
║    1. Collect 10–15g of dry material (aim for variety — several  ║
║       different fibres give different burn rates; finer catches  ║
║       faster, coarser sustains longer).                          ║
║    2. Work between palms in circular motion for 30 seconds.      ║
║       Goal: separate fibres, increase surface area.              ║
║    3. Shape into a rough cup/nest: 10 cm diameter, walls 2 cm   ║
║       thick, hollow centre 5 cm dia. Keep it loose — airflow    ║
║       is critical.                                               ║
║    4. Use immediately or store in sealed pouch.                  ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  SKILLS: None. 5 minutes of observation and practice.           ║
║  CYCLE TIME: 3 minutes to prepare one nest from collected material║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
