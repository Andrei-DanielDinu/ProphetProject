# R150 — PCR Thermocycler and CRISPR-Cas9 Endonuclease

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R150 · PCR THERMOCYCLER & CRISPR-CAS9 GENE EDITING       ║
║  End product: Targeted Double-Strand DNA Cleavage & Sequence Edit║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHY THIS IS THE MOLECULAR SCALPEL OF BIOTECHNOLOGY              ║
║    CRISPR-Cas9 enables programmable editing of genetic code in   ║
║    living cells (crops, livestock, human therapeutic repair).    ║
║    Components:                                                   ║
║    1. Cas9 Endonuclease: molecular enzyme that cuts DNA double   ║
║       helix like scissors.                                       ║
║    2. Single Guide RNA (sgRNA): 20-nucleotide synthetic RNA      ║
║       address that matches the target genomic sequence.          ║
║    Cas9 binds sgRNA, searches cellular genome, finds matching    ║
║    sequence next to a PAM motif (5'-NGG-3'), unwinds DNA, and   ║
║    makes a precise, targeted double-strand break at position -3! ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PART 1: PELTIER PCR THERMAL CYCLER HARDWARE                     ║
║    • Solid-state bismuth telluride (Bi₂Te₃) Peltier thermoelectric║
║      heat pump mounted to aluminum sample block [→ R080/R101].   ║
║    • Microprocessor-controlled PID thermal loop [→ R130].        ║
║    • Standard 30-cycle DNA amplification protocol:               ║
║      - 95°C for 30s: Denaturation (DNA double helix unzips).     ║
║      - 55°C for 30s: Primer Annealing (synthetic primers bind).  ║
║      - 72°C for 60s: Extension (Taq polymerase synthesizes copy).║
║    • Amplifies 1 single DNA molecule into 1,000,000,000 copies!  ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PART 2: CRISPR RIBONUCLEOPROTEIN (RNP) IN-VITRO ASSEMBLY        ║
║                                                                  ║
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║  A) Recombinant Cas9 protein (30 μM in HEPES buffer, pH 7.5):    ║
║     Expressed in E. coli culture, purified via nickel-NTA        ║
║     affinity chromatography column [→ R020/Manual 05].           ║
║  B) Synthetic Single Guide RNA (sgRNA, 100 μM):                  ║
║     Synthesized via T7 RNA polymerase in-vitro transcription.    ║
║  C) Cleavage buffer: 20 mM HEPES, 100 mM KCl, 5 mM MgCl₂,        ║
║     1 mM DTT, 5% glycerol.                                       ║
║                                                                  ║
║  REACTION PROTOCOL                                               ║
║  1. Incubate 1 μL sgRNA with 1 μL Cas9 in 8 μL cleavage buffer   ║
║     at 25°C for 10 minutes to form active Cas9-RNP complex.     ║
║  2. Add 200 ng target PCR-amplified DNA fragment.                ║
║  3. Incubate at 37°C for 30 minutes in water bath.               ║
║  4. Add Proteinase K, heat to 55°C for 10 min to digest Cas9.    ║
║  5. Run Agarose Gel Electrophoresis: ethidium bromide staining   ║
║     reveals the original DNA band cleanly cleaved into two exact ║
║     predicted daughter fragments! Genetic engineering confirmed. ║
```
