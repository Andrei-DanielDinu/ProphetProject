# R320 — Lithium-Ion Secondary Battery Cell

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R320 · RECHARGEABLE LITHIUM-ION CELL (18650 CYLINDRICAL) ║
║  End product: 1 × 18650 Li-Ion Cell (3.7V nominal, 3,000 mAh)    ║
║  Specific Energy: 250 Wh/kg (5× higher than lead-acid R081!)     ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHY LITHIUM-ION UNLOCKS MOBILE TECHNOLOGY & VEHICLES            ║
║    Lithium is the lightest metal (atomic weight 6.94 g/mol) and  ║
║    the most electropositive (-3.04V vs standard hydrogen).       ║
║    John Goodenough (1980) and Akira Yoshino (1985) pioneered     ║
║    "rocking-chair" intercalation: lithium ions (Li⁺) de-intercalate║
║    from a transition metal oxide cathode and slip reversibly     ║
║    into layers of graphite anode without plating metallic lithium!║
║    It enables smartphones, laptops, drones, and electric cars.   ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║                                                                  ║
║  A) CATHODE ACTIVE MATERIAL: Lithium Cobalt Oxide (LiCoO₂)      ║
║     • Synthesized by solid-state reaction: stoichiometric        ║
║       Lithium carbonate (Li₂CO₃, from salt brine evaporation) +  ║
║       Cobalt(II,III) oxide (Co₃O₄, from Eastern Desert ores)     ║
║       calcined in air at 850°C for 12 hours.                     ║
║     • Slurry coated onto 15 μm aluminum foil current collector:  ║
║       90% LiCoO₂ + 5% carbon black + 5% PVDF binder in NMP.      ║
║                                                                  ║
║  B) ANODE ACTIVE MATERIAL: Mesoporous Synthetic Graphite         ║
║     • Pure graphitized petroleum coke powder [→ R025 / R120].    ║
║     • Coated onto 10 μm high-purity copper foil current collector║
║       using water-based SBR/CMC rubber binder.                   ║
║                                                                  ║
║  C) NON-AQUEOUS LIQUID ELECTROLYTE                               ║
║     • ⚠ CANNOT USE WATER! Lithium reacts explosively with water. ║
║     • 1.0 Molar Lithium Hexafluorophosphate salt (LiPF₆)         ║
║       dissolved in anhydrous organic carbonate solvent blend     ║
║       (50:50 Ethylene Carbonate : Dimethyl Carbonate).           ║
║     • Water content must be < 10 parts per million (PPM)!        ║
║                                                                  ║
║  D) MICROPOROUS POLYOLEFIN SEPARATOR                             ║
║     • 16 μm biaxially stretched polyethylene/polypropylene film: ║
║       Sub-micron pores allow Li⁺ ions to pass freely, while      ║
║       physically preventing electrical contact between foils.    ║
║     • "Thermal shutdown" feature: melts closed at 130°C if       ║
║       shorted, shutting off ion flow to prevent thermal runaway! ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  MANUFACTURING PROTOCOL (In Dry Room, Dew Point < -45°C)         ║
║    1. Slit cathode, separator, and anode into 58 mm wide strips. ║
║    2. Automated Winding: Wind strips into tight spiral "jellyroll"║
║    3. Insert jellyroll into deep-drawn nickel-plated steel can   ║
║       (18 mm diameter × 65 mm tall = "18650" standard).          ║
║    4. Spot-weld nickel tabs to can base (-) and top cap (+).     ║
║    5. Vacuum electrolyte filling: inject 5 mL electrolyte.       ║
║    6. Seal top cap with polypropylene gasket in crimping press.  ║
║    7. Formation cycling: Charge slowly at 0.1C to 4.20V:         ║
║       Builds protective Solid Electrolyte Interphase (SEI) layer ║
║       on graphite. Cell ready for 1,000 deep recharge cycles!    ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
