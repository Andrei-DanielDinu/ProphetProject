# R172 — Combined Sanitary Sewer & Activated Sludge Wastewater Plant

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R172 · ACTIVATED SLUDGE WASTEWATER TREATMENT PLANT       ║
║  Capacity: 40,000 m³/day municipal sewage (150,000 population)   ║
║  BOD₅ reduction: >95%, Suspended Solids: <10 mg/L, Biogas energy ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PURPOSE IN RECURSIVE CHAIN                                      ║
║    The civilizational sanitation & environmental prerequisite:   ║
║    • Total elimination of urban sewage contamination in rivers   ║
║    • Eradication of water-borne epidemics across urban centers   ║
║    • Renewable methane biogas energy generation and fertilizer   ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  RECURSIVE INGREDIENT & TOOL DEPENDENCIES                        ║
║    1. Vitrified Clay / Reinforced Concrete Sewer Pipes: 60 km    ║
║       [→ R020, R160, R161]                                       ║
║    2. Mechanical Step Screens & Vortex Grit Chambers: 1 set      ║
║       [→ R036 stainless steel, R040]                             ║
║    3. Fine-Bubble Membrane Diffusers: 2,500 pcs [→ R164 rubber]  ║
║    4. Multi-Stage Centrifugal Air Blowers (150 kW): 3 pcs [→ R284]║
║    5. Submersible Sludge Recirculation Pumps: 6 pcs [→ R284]     ║
║    6. Concrete Anaerobic Digester Tanks (6,000 m³): 2 [→ R161]   ║
║    7. Gas Engine Cogeneration Generator (500 kW): 1 [→ R240]     ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 1: COLLECTION & PRIMARY PHYSICAL TREATMENT                ║
║    1a. Gravity sewer network: Lay vitrified clay and reinforced  ║
║        concrete pipes [→ R161] with minimum self-cleansing slope ║
║        (velocity >0.75 m/s) to convey domestic sewage by gravity.║
║    1b. Automatic bar screens: 6 mm slot width stainless steel    ║
║        screens [→ R036] trap rags, plastics, and coarse solids;  ║
║        screenings are dewatered and incinerated [→ R021].        ║
║    1c. Vortex grit chamber: Tangential flow causes heavy sand    ║
║        and mineral grit to settle to bottom cone within 45 sec;   ║
║        removes 95% of abrasive mineral particles >0.2 mm.        ║
║    1d. Primary circular clarifier: 2 hours retention time. 60%   ║
║        of raw suspended solids settle into hopper bottom as raw  ║
║        sludge; surface skimmer skims floating grease and oils.   ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 2: BIOLOGICAL SECONDARY TREATMENT (ACTIVATED SLUDGE)      ║
║    2a. Aeration basin: Primary settled sewage enters plug-flow   ║
║        concrete aeration basins where it mixes with recycled     ║
║        active biological floc (Mixed Liquor Suspended Solids     ║
║        MLSS = 3,500 mg/L).                                       ║
║    2b. Fine-bubble diffused aeration: Submerged perforated       ║
║        synthetic rubber discs [→ R164] pump millions of 2 mm     ║
║        air micro-bubbles; maintains dissolved $O_2$ at 2.0 mg/L. ║
║    2c. Microbial degradation: Billions of aerobic heterotrophic  ║
║        bacteria (*Zoogloea ramigera*, *Pseudomonas*) consume     ║
║        soluble organic matter (BOD / Biochemical Oxygen Demand): ║
║        Organics + O₂ + NH₄⁺ ──► New Bacterial Biomass + CO₂ + H₂O║
║    2d. Biological nitrification: Autotrophic bacteria oxidize    ║
║        toxic ammonia to nitrate:                                 ║
║        NH₄⁺ + 2 O₂ ──► NO₃⁻ + 2 H⁺ + H₂O.                        ║
║    2e. Secondary clarifier: Mixed liquor enters final clarifiers.║
║        Active microbial flocs settle out into dense blanket.     ║
║        90% of settled sludge is pumped back to the aeration basin║
║        head (Return Activated Sludge / RAS) to sustain culture.  ║
║    2f. Crystal clear, non-turbid effluent overflows weirs;       ║
║        disinfected with UV lamps [→ R135] or chlorine [→ R171].  ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 3: ANAEROBIC SLUDGE DIGESTION & BIOGAS ENERGY             ║
║    3a. Waste activated sludge + primary raw sludge are thickened ║
║        to 5% dry solids and pumped into sealed concrete anaerobic║
║        digester tanks [→ R161].                                  ║
║    3b. Heat digester to mesophilic 37°C using waste engine heat; ║
║        mix with draft-tube impellers. Retain for 25 days.        ║
║    3c. Anaerobic digestion cascade:                              ║
║        • Acidogenic bacteria convert fats/proteins into acetate. ║
║        • Methanogenic archaea (*Methanothrix*) convert acetate:  ║
║          CH₃COOH ──► CH₄ (Methane, 65%) + CO₂ (35%).             ║
║    3d. Collect biogas in floating-cover gas holders [→ R040].    ║
║        Scrub H₂S with iron oxide sponge [→ R040]; burn methane   ║
║        in gas engine generator [→ R240] to generate 500 kW       ║
║        electricity—making the entire treatment plant net-zero!   ║
║    3e. Dewatered stabilized sludge digestate is pasteurized and  ║
║        applied to farmland as Class-A rich nitrogenous humus.    ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  QUALITY CONTROL METRICS                                         ║
║    • Influent BOD₅: 300 mg/L ──► Effluent BOD₅: <10 mg/L (97%).  ║
║    • Suspended Solids: <10 mg/L (drinking-water river discharge).║
║    • Pathogen destruction in digestate: >99.999% destruction.    ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
