# R052 — Sodium Hydroxide Lye (Caustic Soda Solution)

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R052 · SODIUM HYDROXIDE (NaOH) LYE SOLUTION (~30%)       ║
║  End product: 3 L of concentrated caustic lye solution           ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PURPOSE IN RECURSIVE CHAIN                                      ║
║    Strong base required for:                                     ║
║    • Saponification of fats into soap [→ R050]                   ║
║    • Digestion of plant basts into paper & chemical pulp         ║
║    • Wolframite ore fusion for tungsten extraction [→ R091]      ║
║    • Electrolysis chemical baths                                 ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  CHEMICAL MECHANISM                                              ║
║    Na₂CO₃ + Ca(OH)₂ ──► 2 NaOH (aqueous) + CaCO₃ (solid insol)   ║
║    (Natron + Slaked Lime ──► Sodium Hydroxide + Insoluble Chalk) ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║  A) 2.0 kg Anhydrous Natron (or 5.0 kg raw decahydrate) [→ R000-E]║
║  B) 1.2 kg Freshly calcined quicklime (CaO) [→ R027]             ║
║  C) 5.0 L Water [→ R000-H]                                       ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  TOOLS                                                           ║
║    • 10 L Clay mixing pot [→ R020]                               ║
║    • Wooden stirring paddle [→ R000-I]                           ║
║    • Settling jars and filtration cloth                          ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PROCESS                                                         ║
║  1. Slake quicklime: Add 1.2 kg CaO to 2 L water. Reaction boils ║
║     vigorously, yielding creamy calcium hydroxide slurry Ca(OH)₂.║
║  2. In separate pot, dissolve 2.0 kg natron in 3 L hot water.    ║
║  3. Pour hot natron solution into the calcium hydroxide slurry.  ║
║  4. Stir constantly for 30 minutes at 80°C. Heavy chalky white   ║
║     calcium carbonate precipitates out of solution.              ║
║  5. Let stand for 4 hours. Calcium carbonate settles to bottom.  ║
║  6. Siphon or decant crystal-clear caustic NaOH liquid off top.  ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  SAFETY: Extremely caustic! Causes blindness on eye contact.     ║
║  Store in stoppered ceramic containers. Do not use metal pots.   ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
