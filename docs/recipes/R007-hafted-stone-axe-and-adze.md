# R007 — Hafted Ground Stone Axe and Adze

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R007 · HAFTED GROUND STONE AXE & ADZE                   ║
║  End product: 1 × Polished Stone Felling Axe (mass 1.8 kg)       ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHY YOU NEED THIS                                               ║
║    Before copper axes exist, you must fell trees for charcoal,   ║
║    mine timber, and kiln construction. A flint axe chips easily. ║
║    A polished ground stone axe made of basalt or diorite does    ║
║    NOT chip on impact — it acts like a wedge with immense energy.║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║  A) 1 × Fine-grained basalt, diorite, or jadeite river cobble   ║
║     [■ TERMINAL]. Mass: 1.0 kg, Length: 16 cm, Width: 8 cm.      ║
║  B) 1 × Heavy seasoned acacia branch with root club (Axe Haft)   ║
║     [→ R000-I] ■ TERMINAL. Length: 70 cm, Head diameter: 10 cm. ║
║  C) 1.5 m Rawhide binding thong [→ R000-K]                       ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PROCESS                                                         ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 1 — PECKING TO SHAPE (4 hours)                             ║
║    1a. Peck cobble with sharp quartzite hammerstone (R001)       ║
║        into a tapered symmetrical celt wedge.                    ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 2 — WET GRINDING & POLISHING (8 hours)                     ║
║    2a. Rub celt back and forth across coarse sandstone slab      ║
║        with water and quartz sand abrasive.                      ║
║    2b. Form a convex, razor-sharp cutting bit (radius: 4 cm,     ║
║        included bevel angle: 60° for durability against wood).   ║
║    2c. Polish on fine siltstone to mirror finish. Polishing      ║
║        removes micro-fractures, preventing cracks from spreading.║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 3 — HAFTING IN MORTISED CLUB                               ║
║    3a. Burn or bore a tapered socket into the club end of handle.║
║    3b. Insert stone celt: the taper locks tighter with every blow║
║        struck into timber.                                       ║
║    3c. Wrap wet rawhide binding around haft head. Let dry 48h.   ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PERFORMANCE: Fells a 20 cm acacia tree in 15 minutes.           ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
