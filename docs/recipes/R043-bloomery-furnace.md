# R043 — Bloomery Furnace (Iron Smelting Shaft)

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R043 · BLOOMERY FURNACE                                  ║
║  End product: 1 operational bloomery shaft furnace, 1,300°C cap ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHAT YOU GET                                                    ║
║    A tall clay-lined shaft furnace taller and narrower than the ║
║    copper kiln (R021). The tall column creates a temperature     ║
║    gradient: pre-heating zone (top), reduction zone (middle),   ║
║    bloom zone (bottom tuyere level).                             ║
║    Lifetime: 20–30 runs before major re-lining required.        ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║  A) 1,000 × mudbrick, 36×18×9 cm      [→ R022]                 ║
║  B) 200 kg refractory clay lining mix  [→ R023]                 ║
║  C) 100 L water (construction)         [→ R000-H] ■ TERM.      ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  DIMENSIONS (critical — different from copper kiln)             ║
║                                                                  ║
║  EXTERNAL: 80 cm × 80 cm base, 1.8 m tall.                     ║
║  INTERNAL: 30 cm ID shaft, 90 cm tall working zone.             ║
║  WALL: 25 cm mudbrick + 5 cm refractory lining inside.          ║
║                                                                  ║
║  Cross-section diagram:                                          ║
║                                                                  ║
║    ══════════════  ← open top (charge loaded from top)           ║
║    │            │                                                ║
║    │ PRE-HEAT   │ 30 cm ID shaft                                 ║
║    │   ZONE     │ Ore preheated by ascending hot gases           ║
║    │            │                                                ║
║    │────────────│                                                ║
║    │ REDUCTION  │ CO-rich zone. Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂       ║
║    │   ZONE     │ 900–1,100°C here                               ║
║    │────────────│                                                ║
║    │ ► TUYERE   │ ← 4 cm ID clay pipe, tip 8 cm from wall       ║
║    │            │   angle: 20° below horizontal                  ║
║    │  BLOOM     │ ← 1,200–1,300°C here                           ║
║    │  ZONE      │ Iron sinters into bloom                        ║
║    │  slag pool │ ← slag (liquid) pools at base                  ║
║    ╘══╤═════════╛                                                ║
║       │ 3cm slag/extraction tap hole (5 cm above base)          ║
║       └────► clay-plugged when not tapping                       ║
║                                                                  ║
║  KEY DIFFERENCE from copper kiln:                               ║
║    • Narrow shaft (30 cm ID vs 60 cm for kiln) concentrates    ║
║      heat at tuyere zone, allows tall charge column.            ║
║    • No perforated floor (charge descends continuously).        ║
║    • Single tuyere (not bilateral like big kiln).              ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  TUYERE SPECIFICATIONS (critical)                               ║
║    • Material: refractory clay (R023) coiled + fired.           ║
║    • Inner diameter: 4 cm exactly. Too small: insufficient air. ║
║      Too large: uneven air distribution, cool spots.            ║
║    • Length: 25 cm (passes through 25 cm thick wall).           ║
║    • Tip offset from inner wall: 8 cm (tips protruding too far  ║
║      into furnace accumulate slag and block).                   ║
║    • Angle: 20° below horizontal (directs air downward          ║
║      toward bloom zone, not across the shaft horizontally).     ║
║    • Replace tuyere every 5 runs (clay tip erodes from slag     ║
║      contact). Pre-make 10 spares.                              ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PROCESS — CONSTRUCTION (5 days)                                ║
║    Day 1: Excavate base, compact rammed earth 20 cm. Lay 2      ║
║      brick courses (foundation).                                ║
║    Day 2: Build mudbrick shaft walls 1.8 m tall. Leave tuyere   ║
║      hole at correct height. Leave tap hole at base.            ║
║    Day 3: Apply refractory lining inside (R023). 2 coats.       ║
║    Day 4: Install tuyere pipe. Seal gap. Set tap hole clay plug.║
║    Day 5: 2 curing fires (200°C then 600°C). Inspect. Repair.  ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  SKILLS: Same as kiln construction (R021). 1 week training.    ║
║  CONSTRUCTION TIME: 5 days (crew of 4).                        ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
