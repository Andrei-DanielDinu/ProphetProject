# R045 — Iron Blacksmith Tongs (Riveted Wrought Iron)

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R045 · WROUGHT IRON BLACKSMITH TONGS                    ║
║  End product: 1 × Pair of Flat-Jaw Forged Iron Tongs (55 cm)     ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHY THIS MATTERS                                                ║
║    Hardwood tongs burn out after 15 heats. Iron blacksmith tongs ║
║    last a lifetime, hold red-hot workpieces with immovable grip, ║
║    and can be dipped in water repeatedly without degradation.    ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║  A) 1.2 kg Wrought iron bar [→ R040]                             ║
║     (Two 500g bars for reins/jaws + 200g for pivot rivet).       ║
║  B) 3 kg Charcoal for forge fire [→ R025]                        ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  TOOLS                                                           ║
║    • Forge & pot bellows [→ R021 / R026]                         ║
║    • Granite anvil stone [→ R002]                                ║
║    • Iron blacksmith hammer [→ R046] (or quartzite maul R001)    ║
║    • Initial hardwood tongs [→ R044]                             ║
║    • Flint or hot-iron cutting chisel [→ R005 / R047]            ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PROCESS                                                         ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 1 — FORGING THE JAW AND BOSS (Repeat for each rein)        ║
║    1a. Heat 500g wrought iron bar to bright orange (950°C).      ║
║    1b. Place on anvil edge. Hammer first 5 cm flat → Jaw (2.5 cm ║
║        wide × 1.0 cm thick).                                     ║
║    1c. Rotate bar 90° clockwise. Hammer next 4 cm section flat   ║
║        → Boss (the pivot zone, 3.0 cm wide × 0.8 cm thick).      ║
║    1d. Draw out the remaining 40 cm into a comfortable octagonal ║
║        or round tapered rein (handle, 1.2 cm tapering to 0.8 cm).║
║    1e. Repeat steps 1a–1d for the second arm, ensuring it is a   ║
║        mirror image so jaws align when bosses cross.             ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 2 — HOT PUNCHING THE RIVET HOLE                            ║
║    2a. Heat boss section of both arms to yellow heat (1,050°C).  ║
║    2b. Drive a tapered iron punch through the center of each boss║
║        until a clean 10 mm round hole is formed.                 ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 3 — RIVETING THE PIVOT PIN                                 ║
║    3a. Forge a 10 mm diameter × 4 cm long iron pin from scrap.   ║
║    3b. Align both tong arms. Insert red-hot rivet pin through    ║
║        both boss holes.                                          ║
║    3c. Rest rivet head on anvil. Hammer the protruding end with  ║
║        rapid blows (upsetting) into a dome head.                 ║
║    3d. Open and close tongs continuously while rivet cools so it ║
║        does not freeze solid. Quench in water.                   ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  VERIFICATION: Jaws must clamp a 10 mm flat bar parallel with no ║
║  twist or wobble under full hand grip pressure.                  ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
