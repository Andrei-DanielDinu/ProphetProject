# R092 — Piston Vacuum Pump (Reciprocating High-Vacuum Pump)

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R092 · RECIPROCATING PISTON VACUUM PUMP                  ║
║  End product: 1 × Dual-Stage Vacuum Pump (10⁻² to 10⁻³ mbar)     ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PURPOSE IN RECURSIVE CHAIN                                      ║
║    Evacuates air from glass envelopes to create high vacuum for: ║
║    • Vacuum Tube Triodes [→ R090]                                ║
║    • Cathode ray tubes & particle accelerators                   ║
║    • Vacuum metallurgy & distillation                            ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║  A) Cast iron cylinder & piston (50 mm bore × 150 mm stroke)     ║
║     [→ R071 / R073]                                              ║
║  B) Precision ground glass ball-valves & seats [→ R060 / R061]   ║
║  C) Heavy vacuum oil seal (degassed mineral/castor oil) [→ R051] ║
║  D) Phosphor bronze valve return springs [→ R031]                ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PROCESS                                                         ║
║  1. Lap cylinder bore to mirror finish using fine emery slurry.  ║
║  2. Fit piston with oil-submerged leather/rubber cup packing.    ║
║  3. Install gravity-drop glass ball check valves at intake/head. ║
║  4. Flood cylinder head with 5 cm oil reservoir: prevents any    ║
║     atmospheric air leaking past shaft during intake stroke.     ║
║  5. Connect intake port via thick-walled lead/glass tubing to    ║
║     vacuum chamber. Operate at 60 strokes/min via motor/treadle. ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  VACUUM ATTAINED: Pulls down to 0.05 mbar in 3-liter chamber.    ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
