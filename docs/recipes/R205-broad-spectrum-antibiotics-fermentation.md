# R205 — Broad-Spectrum Antibiotics (Streptomycin & Tetracycline Fermentation)

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R205 · BROAD-SPECTRUM ANTIBIOTIC FERMENTATION            ║
║  End product: 5.0 kg Crystalline Tetracycline Hydrochloride      ║
║  Activity: Kills Gram-positive and Gram-negative bacteria,       ║
║  rickettsiae, chlamydiae, and mycoplasmas (30S ribosomal arrest) ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PURPOSE IN RECURSIVE CHAIN                                      ║
║    The life-saving pharmaceutical prerequisite for:              ║
║    • Curing pulmonary tuberculosis, plague, cholera, and typhus  ║
║    • Treating post-surgical and battle-trauma wound infections   ║
║    • Eradicating resistant bacterial outbreaks across populations║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  RECURSIVE INGREDIENT & TOOL DEPENDENCIES                        ║
║    1. Pure Seed Culture: *Streptomyces aureofaciens* / *griseus* ║
║       [→ Isolated from cultivated soil samples on starch agar]   ║
║    2. Fermentation Growth Medium (per 1,000 L batch):            ║
║       • Corn steep liquor (nitrogen/nutrient source): 30 kg      ║
║       • Starch / Glucose hydrolysate: 40 kg [→ grain processing] ║
║       • Ammonium sulfate ((NH₄)₂SO₄): 5 kg [→ R055, R053]        ║
║       • Calcium carbonate (CaCO₃ buffer): 8 kg [→ R027 limestone]║
║       • Soybean meal flour: 15 kg [→ agriculture]                ║
║    3. Sterile Compressed Air: 2,000 m³ [→ R070, HEPA filter]     ║
║    4. Active Decolorizing Bone Char / Carbon: 10 kg [→ R042]     ║
║    5. Butanol / Methyl Isobutyl Ketone Solvent: 200 L [→ R054]   ║
║    6. Hydrochloric Acid (HCl): 5 L [→ R053]                      ║
║    7. Stainless Steel Jacketed Stirred Fermenter (1,500 L): 1    ║
║       [→ R036, R072]                                             ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 1: STERILIZATION & INOCULUM EXPANSION                     ║
║    1a. Prepare 1,000 L nutrient broth in 316L stainless steel    ║
║        fermenter [→ R036]. Steam sterilize in situ at 121°C      ║
║        (1.1 bar steam [→ R070]) for 45 minutes.                  ║
║    1b. Cool under positive sterile air pressure to 28°C.         ║
║    1c. Inoculate with 50 L active vegetative seed culture of     ║
║        *Streptomyces aureofaciens* grown in shake flasks.        ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 2: SUBMERGED AEROBIC FERMENTATION                         ║
║    2a. Sparge sterile compressed air continuously through bottom ║
║        bubble ring at 0.8 VVM (volume air per volume liquid per  ║
║        minute). Dissolved oxygen >40% saturation.                ║
║    2b. Agitate at 180 RPM with dual Rushton turbine impellers    ║
║        driven by electric motor [→ R284].                        ║
║    2c. Maintain fermentation broth temperature strictly at 28°C  ║
║        via cooling water jacket. Maintain pH at 6.0 to 6.8 with  ║
║        automated sterile NH₄OH and H₂SO₄ dosing [→ R053, R283].  ║
║    2d. Ferment for 120 hours. Actinobacteria mycelia produce and ║
║        secrete chlortetracycline/tetracycline into broth.        ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 3: DOWNSTREAM FILTRATION, EXTRACTION & CRYSTALLIZATION    ║
║    3a. Acidify broth to pH 1.5 with concentrated HCl to dissolve ║
║        all bound antibiotic from mycelial cell walls.            ║
║    3b. Filter out cell mass on rotary vacuum drum filter [→ R036]║
║        using diatomaceous earth filter aid.                      ║
║    3c. Adjust clear filtrate to pH 8.5 with ammonia. Add calcium ║
║        chloride: Tetracycline precipitates as an insoluble       ║
║        calcium complex. Filter wet cake.                         ║
║    3d. Dissolve cake in acidified butanol. Add active carbon     ║
║        [→ R042] to adsorb pigments; filter through fine silica.  ║
║    3e. Add concentrated HCl to pH 1.0. Tetracycline hydrochloride║
║        crystallizes out as bright yellow needles.                ║
║    3f. Centrifuge crystals, wash with cold anhydrous ethanol, and║
║        vacuum dry at 40°C.                                       ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  QUALITY CONTROL METRICS                                         ║
║    • Purity: >98.0% Tetracycline HCl (HPLC / spectrophotometry). ║
║    • Biological potency: >950 μg/mg dry basis.                   ║
║    • Sterility & Endotoxin: Pyrogen-free (<0.5 EU/mg).           ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
