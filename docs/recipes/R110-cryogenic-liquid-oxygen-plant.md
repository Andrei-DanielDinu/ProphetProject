# R110 — Cryogenic Air Fractionation (Liquid Oxygen / LOX Plant)

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R110 · CRYOGENIC AIR FRACTIONATION (LOX & LN2 PLANT)     ║
║  End product: 500 kg Liquid Oxygen (LOX, -183°C, 99.5% purity)   ║
║               1,500 kg Liquid Nitrogen (LN2, -196°C) per day     ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHY CRYOGENIC OXYGEN IS ESSENTIAL                               ║
║    • Staged orbital rocketry [→ R111]: RP-1 fuel requires LOX   ║
║      as an oxidizer to produce supersonic thrust in vacuum.      ║
║    • Basic Oxygen Steelmaking (BOS): Blasting pure O₂ into iron  ║
║      converts 100 tonnes of pig iron to steel in 30 minutes!     ║
║    • Liquid Nitrogen (LN2): Inert cryogen for cold traps, vacuum ║
║      shrouds, biology preservation, and Haber-Bosch N₂ feedstock.║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  THERMODYNAMIC CYCLE (Linde-Claude Dual Expansion Cycle)         ║
║    Boiling points at 1 atmosphere:                               ║
║    - Nitrogen (N₂): -195.8°C (77.3 K) — More volatile (boils off)║
║    - Oxygen (O₂):   -182.9°C (90.2 K) — Less volatile (collects) ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PROCESS                                                         ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 1 — AIR PRE-PURIFICATION                                   ║
║    1a. Intake atmospheric air. Filter dust.                      ║
║    1b. Compress to 6 bar in multi-stage reciprocating compressor ║
║        driven by steam engine (R070).                            ║
║    1c. Scrub through solid sodium hydroxide (R052) bed to remove ║
║        all CO₂ (CO₂ would freeze into solid dry ice and plug     ║
║        cryogenic pipes). Dehydrate through silica gel beds.      ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 2 — COMPRESSION & EXPANSION REFRIGERATION                  ║
║    2a. Compress dry, CO₂-free air to 200 bar.                    ║
║    2b. Counter-current heat exchanger: Outgoing cold nitrogen    ║
║        gas precools incoming high-pressure warm air down to -140°C║
║    2c. Expand 70% of high-pressure air through an expansion      ║
║        turbine: air performs mechanical work, shedding internal  ║
║        enthalpy and chilling down to -190°C.                     ║
║    2d. Remaining air expands through Joule-Thomson valve: sudden ║
║        pressure drop from 200 bar to 5 bar liquefies 20% of air! ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 3 — DOUBLE-COLUMN FRACTIONAL DISTILLATION                  ║
║    3a. Liquid air feeds into high-pressure lower column (5 bar). ║
║    3b. Liquid drains into upper low-pressure column (1.3 bar).   ║
║    3c. Nitrogen boils off at top as 99.9% pure N₂ gas (-196°C).  ║
║    3d. Brilliant pale blue Liquid Oxygen (LOX) pools at bottom   ║
║        tray at -183°C.                                           ║
║    3e. Draw off LOX into vacuum-insulated double-walled stainless║
║        steel Dewar storage vessels.                              ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  SAFETY: LOX makes all organic matter (oil, wood, clothing)     ║
║  detonate upon the slightest spark! Keep 100% oil-free!          ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
