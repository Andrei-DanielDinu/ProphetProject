# R160 — Portland Cement and Rotary Clinkering Kiln

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R160 · ORDINARY PORTLAND CEMENT (OPC TYPE 1)             ║
║  End product: 1,000 kg Dry Portland Cement Powder                ║
║  Compressive strength: >35 MPa at 28 days (with sand & aggregate)║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHY PORTLAND CEMENT BUILDS THE MODERN WORLD                     ║
║    Ancient lime plaster (R027) sets by air carbonation (months). ║
║    Roman pozzolana requires rare volcanic ash.                   ║
║    Portland cement (Joseph Aspdin, 1824) is a HYDRAULIC binder:  ║
║    Fired to sintering temperature (1,450°C), calcium silicates   ║
║    form that react chemically with water to crystallize into     ║
║    interlocking calcium silicate hydrate (C-S-H) gels.           ║
║    It sets under water, achieves immense strength in days, and   ║
║    enables monolithic mega-dams, harbors, bridges, and towers.   ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  RAW MEAL INGREDIENTS (Stoichiometric Clinker Blend)             ║
║  A) 1,100 kg High-purity Limestone (CaCO₃, ~75%) [→ R000-J]      ║
║  B) 300 kg Clay, Shale, or River Silt (SiO₂ + Al₂O₃, ~20%)       ║
║     [→ R000-G] (Source of silica and alumina)                    ║
║  C) 50 kg Iron ore powder (Hematite Fe₂O₃, ~5%) [→ R000-D]       ║
║     (Acts as flux to lower clinkering temperature to 1,450°C)    ║
║  D) 40 kg Natural Raw Gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O, 4% retarder) [→ R000] ║
║     (⚠ MANDATORY: Without gypsum, cement flashes into concrete   ║
║     in 2 minutes! Gypsum delays initial set to 90 minutes).      ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  EQUIPMENT: INCLINED ROTARY KILN                                 ║
║    • Steel cylinder (R040): 1.5 m diameter × 25 m long.          ║
║    • Slope: 3% incline. Rotates slowly on rollers at 1 RPM.      ║
║    • Refractory lining: 20 cm high-alumina bricks (R023).        ║
║    • Fired at lower discharge end by pulverized charcoal (R025)  ║
║      blast burner yielding 1,500°C flame.                        ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  MANUFACTURING PROCESS                                           ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 1 — RAW MEAL SLURRY PREPARATION                            ║
║    1a. Grind limestone and clay together in wet ball mill with    ║
║        35% water into ultra-fine slurry (<90 μm grain size).     ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 2 — THE THERMAL CLINKERING CASCADE (Inside Rotary Kiln)    ║
║    2a. Feed slurry into elevated upper cold end of kiln.         ║
║    2b. 100°C–500°C (Drying Zone): Free water evaporates.        ║
║    2c. 600°C–900°C (Calcination Zone):                           ║
║        CaCO₃ ──► CaO + CO₂↑  (Limestone decomposes).             ║
║    2d. 1,200°C–1,450°C (Sintering / Clinkering Zone):            ║
║        Solid-state chemical synthesis occurs:                    ║
║        2 CaO + SiO₂ ──► 2CaO·SiO₂  (Dicalcium Silicate, Belite)  ║
║        3 CaO + SiO₂ ──► 3CaO·SiO₂  (Tricalcium Silicate, Alite)  ║
║        4 CaO + Al₂O₃ + Fe₂O₃ ──► 4CaO·Al₂O₃·Fe₂O₃ (Ferrite)     ║
║    2e. Minerals fuse into marble-sized dark grey nodules called  ║
║        CEMENT CLINKER. Discharge and quench in air cooler.       ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 3 — BALL MILL FINISH GRINDING                              ║
║    3a. Blend 960 kg cooled clinker + 40 kg raw gypsum rock.      ║
║    3b. Pulverize in steel ball mill until Blaine surface area    ║
║        reaches 350 m²/kg (finer than cosmetic face powder!).     ║
║    3c. Bag in multi-ply paper or moisture-proof clay jars.       ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STANDARD MORTAR TEST: 1 part cement + 3 parts sand + 0.5 water: ║
║  Sets in 2 hours. Submerged in water: 7 days = 22 MPa;           ║
║  28 days = 38 MPa (turns into synthetic granite rock!).          ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
