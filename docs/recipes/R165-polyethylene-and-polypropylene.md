# R165 — Polyethylene (HDPE) & Polypropylene (PP) via Ziegler-Natta Catalysis

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R165 · THERMOPLASTIC POLYOLEFINS (HDPE & POLYPROPYLENE)  ║
║  End product: 100 kg High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) Pellets    ║
║  Density: 0.95 g/cm³, Tensile Yield: 30 MPa, Melting Pt: 132°C   ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PURPOSE IN RECURSIVE CHAIN                                      ║
║    The universal thermoplastic prerequisite for:                 ║
║    • Chemical reagent bottles, carboys, and acid piping [→ R053] ║
║    • Electrical wire extrusion insulation and cable jacketing    ║
║    • Battery separators for lead-acid and lithium cells [→ R320] ║
║    • Syringes, IV drip bags, sterile medical consumables [→ R200]║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  RECURSIVE INGREDIENT & TOOL DEPENDENCIES                        ║
║    1. Ethylene Gas (C₂H₄, polymer-grade 99.95%): 102 kg          ║
║       [→ Steam cracking of petroleum naphtha / ethane, R054]     ║
║    2. Hexane Solvent (anhydrous, deoxygenated): 250 L [→ R054]   ║
║    3. Titanium Tetrachloride (TiCl₄ catalyst): 150 g [→ R035]    ║
║    4. Triethylaluminium (Al(C₂H₅)₃ co-catalyst): 300 g           ║
║       [→ Aluminium powder R034 + ethylene + hydrogen]            ║
║    5. Magnesium Ethoxide / Silica Support: 50 g [→ R100]         ║
║    6. Stainless Steel Continuous Stirred-Tank Autoclave: 1 [→ R036]║
║    7. Twin-Screw Extruder and Pelletizer: 1 [→ R072, R036]       ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 1: CATALYST PREPARATION (ZIEGLER-NATTA HETEROGENEOUS)     ║
║    1a. Work under dry argon glovebox atmosphere (<1 ppm O₂, H₂O).║
║    1b. Dissolve 150 g TiCl₄ in 5 L dry hexane. React with 50 g   ║
║        magnesium ethoxide support to form high-activity TiCl₃/MgCl₂║
║        catalytic centers (surface area >200 m²/g).               ║
║    1c. Activate catalyst slurry with 300 g triethylaluminium     ║
║        (AlEt₃) co-catalyst.                                      ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 2: SLURRY-PHASE COORDINATION POLYMERIZATION               ║
║    2a. Charge 250 L dry hexane into 500 L jacketed 316L autoclave.║
║    2b. Inject Ziegler-Natta catalyst slurry.                     ║
║    2c. Pressurize autoclave to 8 bar with high-purity ethylene   ║
║        gas; maintain temperature strictly at 80°C with cooling   ║
║        water jacket:                                             ║
║        n CH₂=CH₂ ──► [─CH₂─CH₂─]ₙ (Linear HDPE).                 ║
║    2d. Inject molecular Hydrogen gas (H₂, 0.2 bar) as chain-     ║
║        transfer agent to precisely control molecular weight      ║
║        (MFI / Melt Flow Index = 2.0 g/10 min).                   ║
║    2e. Ethylene molecules insert coordinates between the Ti      ║
║        metal center and polymer chain end, producing perfectly   ║
║        linear unbranched polyethylene molecules (MW ~200,000).   ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  STAGE 3: FLASHING, DRYING & TWIN-SCREW PELLETIZING              ║
║    3a. Discharge polymer slurry into flash vessel at 1 bar:      ║
║        Hexane solvent vaporizes and is recondensed for reuse.    ║
║    3b. Steam-strip and dry the white fluffy HDPE powder.         ║
║    3c. Add 0.1 wt% phenolic antioxidant (BHT) to prevent heat   ║
║        degradation.                                              ║
║    3d. Feed into twin-screw extruder at 210°C; extrude through   ║
║        die plate into cold water bath; chop into 3 mm pellets.   ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  QUALITY CONTROL METRICS                                         ║
║    • Density: 0.952 g/cm³ (confirms linear high crystallinity).  ║
║    • Tensile strength at yield: 31 MPa; Elongation: >600%.       ║
║    • Chemical resistance: 100% insoluble in hot concentrated    ║
║      acids, alkalis, and organic solvents at room temp.          ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
