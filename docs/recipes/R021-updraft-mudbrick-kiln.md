# R021 — Updraft Mudbrick Kiln

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R021 · UPDRAFT MUDBRICK KILN                            ║
║  End product: 1 permanent fired kiln, internal chamber 60×60×80 ║
║  cm, reaching 1,100°C sustained. Refractory lining.            ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHAT YOU GET                                                    ║
║    A kiln capable of:                                            ║
║    • Firing 100 pottery pieces per batch (900–1,100°C)          ║
║    • Smelting copper ore (1,083°C target)                        ║
║    • Firing refractory bricks and crucibles                      ║
║    • Calcining limestone (900°C for CaO production)             ║
║    Lifetime: 500+ firings if properly maintained.               ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║                                                                  ║
║  A) 2,000 × mudbrick (standard)              [→ R022]          ║
║     Size: 36 × 18 × 9 cm each. Sun-dried, NOT fired.           ║
║     Use Nile alluvial clay + 10% chopped straw binder.          ║
║                                                                  ║
║  B) 500 kg refractory clay mix (kiln lining)  [→ R023]         ║
║     Recipe: 50% kaolin clay + 30% quartz sand + 15% grog        ║
║     (ground old pottery) + 5% chopped straw.                    ║
║     Lining thickness: 5 cm on all interior surfaces.            ║
║                                                                  ║
║  C) 200 kg limestone (for mortar, outer layer) [→ R000-J] TERM.║
║                                                                  ║
║  D) 10 kg fine sand (mortar aggregate)        [→ R000-F] TERM. ║
║                                                                  ║
║  E) Water for mortar and lining               [→ R000-H] TERM. ║
║     Approx. 100 L total for construction.                       ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  TOOLS                                                           ║
║    • Wooden trowel (carved acacia)              [→ R000-I] TERM.║
║    • String line + plumb bob (clay + string)    [→ R000-I] TERM.║
║    • Bucket (leather or woven + clay-sealed)    [→ R024]        ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  CONDITIONS                                                      ║
║    • Site: flat, stable ground. NOT on flood plain (avoid        ║
║      inundation). Minimum 10 m from any thatched structure.      ║
║    • Site prep: excavate 20 cm of topsoil. Compact base.         ║
║    • Construction: in dry weather. Mudbrick mortar needs 3 days  ║
║      to set before the kiln is loaded.                           ║
║    • Initial firing (curing): 2 slow pre-fires before first full ║
║      production fire (drives out moisture from bricks gradually).║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  DIMENSIONS AND LAYOUT                                           ║
║                                                                  ║
║  External: 1.2 m × 1.2 m base, 1.6 m tall.                     ║
║  Wall thickness: 30 cm (double mudbrick layer).                  ║
║  Internal chamber: 60 cm × 60 cm × 80 cm tall.                  ║
║                                                                  ║
║  Cross-section (side view):                                      ║
║                                                                  ║
║    ════════════════════════  ← flue opening (6 cm gap)           ║
║    │  dome/top (arched)   │                                      ║
║    │  ┌─────────────┐     │                                      ║
║    │  │  ware       │     │  ← loading chamber (80 cm tall)      ║
║    │  │  chamber    │     │    (pottery stacked here on props)   ║
║    │  │             │     │                                      ║
║    │  └──────┬──────┘     │                                      ║
║    │         │ perforation│  ← perforated floor (5 cm holes,    ║
║    │         │   shelf    │    20% open area, 10 cm thick)       ║
║    │  ┌──────┴──────┐     │                                      ║
║    │  │  FIREBOX    │     │  ← firebox (30 cm tall)              ║
║    │  │  (fuel here)│     │    hardwood + charcoal fuel          ║
║    └──┴─────────────┴─────┘                                      ║
║            ↑                                                     ║
║       stoke hole (front):                                        ║
║       30 cm wide × 30 cm tall arch opening                       ║
║                                                                  ║
║  Top plan view:                                                  ║
║    ┌──────────────────────────┐                                  ║
║    │        mudbrick wall     │                                  ║
║    │   ┌──────────────────┐   │                                  ║
║    │   │  ware chamber    │   │  60 × 60 cm interior            ║
║    │   │                  │   │                                  ║
║    │   └──────────────────┘   │                                  ║
║    │                          │                                  ║
║    └──────────────────────────┘                                  ║
║         ↑ stoke hole (front face)                                ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PROCESS — CONSTRUCTION                                          ║
║                                                                  ║
║  PHASE 1 — FOUNDATION (Day 1, 2 hours, 4 workers)               ║
║    1a. Excavate site 20 cm deep, 1.5 m × 1.5 m area.            ║
║    1b. Fill with 20 cm rammed earth. Compact with flat stone.    ║
║    1c. Mark out 1.2 × 1.2 m outer wall footprint with string.   ║
║                                                                  ║
║  PHASE 2 — FIREBOX WALLS (Day 1–2, 8 hours, 4 workers)          ║
║    2a. Mix mortar: 2 parts clay + 1 part fine sand + water.      ║
║        Consistency: stiff peanut butter.                         ║
║    2b. Lay first course of mudbricks in rectangle per plan.      ║
║        Stagger joints (not aligned = stronger). Mortar joints    ║
║        10 mm thick.                                              ║
║    2c. Build firebox walls 30 cm high (3–4 courses of brick).   ║
║    2d. Leave stoke hole arch: 30 cm wide × 30 cm tall opening   ║
║        in front face. Arch key: lean bricks inward, support      ║
║        with temporary clay-packed stick formwork until mortar    ║
║        sets (24h).                                               ║
║    2e. Build perforated floor across firebox top:                ║
║        Lay bricks with 5 cm gaps between them (the holes).       ║
║        Cover 80% of area with bricks, leave 20% as holes.        ║
║                                                                  ║
║  PHASE 3 — WARE CHAMBER WALLS (Day 2–3, 8 hours, 4 workers)     ║
║    3a. Continue mudbrick walls above perforated floor.           ║
║        Build 80 cm tall chamber.                                 ║
║    3b. Leave 6 cm flue gap at top of back wall (for draft).     ║
║    3c. Dome the top: lean bricks inward each course, corbelling. ║
║        OR: flat top with 3 large flat stone slabs across top.    ║
║                                                                  ║
║  PHASE 4 — REFRACTORY LINING (Day 3–4, 6 hours, 2 workers)      ║
║    4a. Mix refractory lining: 50% kaolin + 30% quartz sand +    ║
║        15% grog + 5% chopped straw. Add water to stiff paste.   ║
║    4b. Apply 5 cm thick lining to all interior surfaces:         ║
║        firebox walls, perforated floor underside, ware chamber   ║
║        walls. Apply in 2 layers: 3 cm base, dry 24h, then 2 cm. ║
║    4c. Smooth with wet hand. Interior should be smooth.          ║
║                                                                  ║
║  PHASE 5 — CURING FIRES (Day 5–7)                                ║
║    5a. Cure fire 1: load firebox with dry grass and small sticks.║
║        Light. Burn at low temperature (~200°C) for 2 hours.      ║
║        Cool 12h. Inspect for cracks: press damp clay into any   ║
║        cracks found.                                             ║
║    5b. Cure fire 2: hardwood fire, 1 hour to reach ~600°C.       ║
║        Cool 12h. Inspect again.                                  ║
║    5c. After 2 cure fires: kiln ready for production.           ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PROCESS — OPERATION (per firing cycle)                          ║
║                                                                  ║
║  LOAD:                                                           ║
║    • Stack pottery on refractory props (small clay wads).        ║
║    • Leave 3 cm gap between all pieces for heat circulation.     ║
║    • Place temperature test tiles at different heights           ║
║      (small clay balls deform at known temps — calibrate first). ║
║                                                                  ║
║  FIRE SCHEDULE:                                                  ║
║    Hour 0–1: Small fire, dry sticks. Raise slowly to 200°C.     ║
║    Hour 1–2: Add more fuel. Reach 500°C (black = invisible,     ║
║               pottery turning faint red in dark).               ║
║    Hour 2–3: Add hardwood charcoal. Reach 800–900°C (cherry-    ║
║               red visible through stoke hole).                  ║
║    Hour 3–4: Maximum fire. Add charcoal. Target 1,000–1,100°C. ║
║               Orange-white visible in chamber.                   ║
║    Hour 4–5: Bank fire (reduce but maintain). Hold temperature. ║
║    Hour 5+:  Stop stoking. Close stoke hole partially with wet  ║
║               brick. Cool slowly.                               ║
║    After 8h: Open kiln. Remove ware.                            ║
║                                                                  ║
║  FUEL PER FIRING: 50 kg dry hardwood + 20 kg charcoal           ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  SKILLS                                                          ║
║    • Mudbrick laying: 2 hours practice                           ║
║    • Refractory lining application: 1 day practice              ║
║    • Fire management: 3 firings to master temperature control    ║
║    CONSTRUCTION TIME: 7 days (1 crew of 4)                      ║
║    PER BATCH: 5–6 hours active stoking + 8h cool-down           ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PRODUCTION TARGETS                                              ║
║    • 1 kiln: 1 batch per day (after experienced crew trained).   ║
║    • PROPHET'S PRIORITY: Build 3 kilns in Week 2 simultaneously. ║
║      Kiln 1: pottery production. Kiln 2: copper ore smelting.   ║
║      Kiln 3: calcining limestone for CaO (flux production).     ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
