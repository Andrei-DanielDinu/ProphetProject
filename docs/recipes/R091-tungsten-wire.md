# R091 — Tungsten Wire (Filament and Cathode Grade)

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R091 · TUNGSTEN WIRE (0.1 mm CATHODE GRADE)              ║
║  End product: 5 m Tungsten wire (0.1 mm dia, purity >99.9%)      ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PURPOSE IN RECURSIVE CHAIN                                      ║
║    Essential thermionic emitter cathode for:                     ║
║    • Vacuum Tube Triodes [→ R090]                                ║
║    • Incandescent electric lighting                              ║
║    • X-ray tube targets and electron gun emitters                ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║  A) 500g Wolframite ore ((Fe,Mn)WO₄) [■ TERMINAL — Eastern Des.] ║
║  B) 300g Sodium hydroxide (NaOH) [→ R052]                        ║
║  C) 500 mL Hydrochloric acid (HCl) [→ Manual 05]                 ║
║  D) Hydrogen gas (H₂) [→ R096: Electrolysis of water]            ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PROCESS (Coolidge Powder Metallurgy Process)                    ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 1 — CHEMICAL PURIFICATION                                  ║
║    1a. Fuse wolframite with molten NaOH (R052) at 450°C.         ║
║        Dissolve melt in water → Sodium tungstate (Na₂WO₄).       ║
║    1b. Add HCl: yellow tungstic acid (H₂WO₄) precipitates.       ║
║    1c. Calcine at 500°C → Pure tungsten trioxide (WO₃) powder.   ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 2 — HYDROGEN REDUCTION                                     ║
║    2a. Place WO₃ in refractory tube furnace at 900°C.            ║
║    2b. Pass stream of dry H₂ gas through tube for 3 hours.       ║
║        WO₃ + 3 H₂ ──► W (metal powder) + 3 H₂O (steam).          ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 3 — SINTERING AND HOT SWAGING                              ║
║    3a. Press tungsten powder in hydraulic die at 200 MPa into a  ║
║        6 mm square bar.                                          ║
║    3b. Pass 1,500 Amperes electric current through bar in H₂     ║
║        atmosphere: heats to 3,000°C, sintering grains dense.     ║
║    3c. Hot-swage at 1,400°C through rotary dies down to 1.5 mm.  ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 4 — HOT WIRE DRAWING                                       ║
║    4a. Draw wire through lubricated diamond/carbide dies while   ║
║        heated to 800°C (tungsten is brittle cold, ductile hot).  ║
║    4b. Progressive draws: 1.5 → 1.0 → 0.5 → 0.2 → 0.10 mm.       ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  SPECIFICATION: Melting point: 3,422°C. Work function: 4.54 eV.  ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
