# R033 — Refractory Clay Crucible

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R033 · REFRACTORY CLAY CRUCIBLE                         ║
║  End product: 1 crucible, 2–3 L capacity, withstands 1,300°C   ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHAT YOU GET                                                    ║
║    A dense, fired ceramic vessel for melting metal batches of   ║
║    up to 25 kg copper or 15 kg iron. Handles: thermal shock     ║
║    (from forge to bench), mechanical stress (tongs grip),        ║
║    chemical attack (from molten metal, slag, flux).              ║
║    Lifetime: 10–20 pours before fracture (then recycle as grog).║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║                                                                  ║
║  A) 1.5 kg kaolin refractory clay         [→ R000-G] ■ TERM.  ║
║                                                                  ║
║  B) 500g ground quartz sand, fine          [→ R000-F] ■ TERM.  ║
║                                                                  ║
║  C) 300g ground pottery grog (1–2mm)       [RECYCLE]            ║
║     Crush old fired crucibles/pots to 1–2 mm. First batch:     ║
║     use crushed quartzite instead.                              ║
║                                                                  ║
║  D) 100g ground charcoal powder             [→ R025]            ║
║     Adds: (1) slight reduction in thermal expansion, (2) burns  ║
║     out on first firing leaving microscopic pores (improves     ║
║     thermal shock resistance vs fully dense body).              ║
║                                                                  ║
║  E) Water — to mix to stiff clay           [→ R000-H] ■ TERM.  ║
║     ~300–400 mL per batch.                                       ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  TOOLS                                                           ║
║    • Smooth rounded pebble (burnishing, interior smoothing)      ║
║    • Wooden paddle (outside shaping)                             ║
║    • Kiln for firing to 1,100°C               [→ R021]          ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  CONDITIONS                                                      ║
║    • Wall thickness: 8–10 mm (thinner = lighter but weaker;     ║
║      thicker = more robust but takes longer to heat through).   ║
║    • Drying: 48 hours shade minimum (slow! — thick walls).      ║
║    • Firing: 1,100°C for 2 hours minimum. Cold clay crucible    ║
║      used directly in hot fire WILL shatter.                     ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PROCESS                                                         ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 1 — MIX CLAY BODY                                          ║
║    1a. Combine dry: clay (A) + quartz sand (B) + grog (C) +    ║
║        charcoal powder (D).                                      ║
║    1b. Add water gradually to stiff dough. Wedge 10 min.        ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 2 — FORM CRUCIBLE (pinch method)                           ║
║    2a. Take 500g clay ball. Push thumb into centre.              ║
║    2b. Pinch walls: thumb inside, 4 fingers outside. Rotate.    ║
║    2c. Build up: continually squeeze between thumb and fingers,  ║
║        rotating. Wall thickness: 8–10 mm uniform.               ║
║    2d. Base: 10 mm thick (thicker than walls — takes greatest  ║
║        thermal stress when resting in hot charcoal).             ║
║    2e. Final shape: conical base (1/3 height), cylindrical body  ║
║        (2/3 height). Flared rim (eases pouring). Height: 20 cm. ║
║        Diameter at widest: 15 cm. Rim: 12 cm dia.               ║
║    2f. Smooth interior with wet pebble. Exterior with paddle.   ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 3 — DRY AND FIRE                                           ║
║    3a. Dry 48h shade (not sun — too fast = cracks). Rotate 6-   ║
║        hourly for even drying.                                   ║
║    3b. Pre-fire: place in kiln at low temp (200°C) for 1 hour.  ║
║    3c. Full fire: raise to 1,100°C. Hold 2 hours.               ║
║    3d. Cool in kiln 6 hours. Remove.                             ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 4 — SEASON                                                 ║
║    4a. First use: fill with charcoal inside, heat to 800°C,     ║
║        cool. This "seasons" the pore structure.                  ║
║    4b. After seasoning: ready for metal melting.                 ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  SKILLS: Advanced pottery. 1 week practice.                     ║
║  CYCLE TIME: 2h forming + 48h drying + 4h firing = 3 days.     ║
║  PRODUCTION: Make 20 crucibles at once (kiln holds 20).         ║
║  LIFETIME: 10–20 pours. Keep counting — replace at first crack. ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
