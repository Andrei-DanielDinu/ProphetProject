# R053 — Sulfuric Acid (Lead Chamber Process)

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R053 · SULFURIC ACID (LEAD CHAMBER PROCESS)              ║
║  End product: 50 kg Chamber Acid (H₂SO₄, 65–78% concentration)   ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHY SULFURIC ACID IS THE KING OF CHEMICALS                      ║
║    "The civilizational output of a nation can be measured by its ║
║    consumption of sulfuric acid." (Justus von Liebig).           ║
║    H₂SO₄ is required to make:                                    ║
║    • Nitric acid (HNO₃) for nitro-explosives & propellants       ║
║    • Phosphoric acid & superphosphate fertilizers                ║
║    • Hydrochloric acid (HCl) from salt                           ║
║    • Lead-acid battery electrolyte [→ R081]                      ║
║    • Petroleum refining and uranium ore leaching                 ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  CHEMICAL CASCADE & CATALYSIS                                    ║
║    1. Roasting: S + O₂ ──► SO₂  (or 4 FeS₂ + 11 O₂ ──► 2 Fe₂O₃ + 8 SO₂)║
║    2. Catalytic gas-phase oxidation:                             ║
║       2 NO + O₂ ──► 2 NO₂                                        ║
║       SO₂ + NO₂ + H₂O ──► H₂SO₄ + NO (catalyst regenerated!)     ║
║    The nitrogen oxides act as homogeneous gaseous catalysts,    ║
║    allowing SO₂ to oxidize at ambient chamber temperatures.      ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║  A) 30 kg Elemental sulfur [→ R000: Gemsa Peninsula 27.7°N]      ║
║     OR: 50 kg Iron Pyrite nodules (FeS₂) [→ R000-B]              ║
║  B) 3 kg Potassium nitrate (Saltpeter / KNO₃) [→ R000: Caves]    ║
║  C) Water steam [→ R070 boiler / R000-H]                         ║
║  D) 200 kg Rolled sheet lead (for chamber lining) [→ R081]       ║
║     (Lead resists sulfuric acid by forming protective insoluble  ║
║     lead sulfate PbSO₄ crust; iron would dissolve instantly).    ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PROCESS                                                         ║
║  1. Burn sulfur or roast pyrite in brick furnace with excess air:║
║     produces continuous stream of hot SO₂ gas.                   ║
║  2. In a small side-pot, heat saltpeter (KNO₃) with a cup of     ║
║     concentrated acid to generate nitrogen oxide fumes (NO/NO₂). ║
║  3. Direct SO₂ gas, NO₂ gas, excess air, and low-pressure steam  ║
║     simultaneously into a large lead-lined wooden chamber        ║
║     (2 m × 2 m × 3 m).                                           ║
║  4. Gaseous reactions occur in mid-air: dense, heavy droplets of ║
║     liquid sulfuric acid condense and rain down like a shower    ║
║     onto the lead floor of the chamber.                          ║
║  5. Continuous draw-off: Chamber acid drains from bottom spout   ║
║     at 65–78% concentration (SG 1.6–1.7).                        ║
║  6. To reach 95% concentrated acid: Boil chamber acid in glass   ║
║     retorts (R060) at 330°C: water boils off first.              ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  SAFETY: Extremely corrosive! Acid reacts with organic tissue by ║
║  violent dehydration (chars skin to carbon in seconds).          ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
