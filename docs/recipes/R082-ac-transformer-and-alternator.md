# R082 — AC Alternator and Step-Up Power Transformer

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R082 · AC ALTERNATOR & HIGH-VOLTAGE TRANSFORMER         ║
║  End product: 1 × Single-Phase AC Alternator (240V / 5 kW) +     ║
║               1 × Step-Up Transformer (240V to 2,400V, 5 kVA)   ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHY ALTERNATING CURRENT (AC) WINS THE GRID                      ║
║    Direct current (DC) cannot travel more than 2 km without      ║
║    massive copper cables due to I²R resistive heating losses.    ║
║    Power loss equation: P_loss = I² × R.                         ║
║    If you step up voltage by 10× (240V → 2,400V), the current    ║
║    drops by 10× for the same power.                             ║
║    Losses drop by (10)² = 100 TIMES!                             ║
║    Transformers allow transmitting power 50 km from the Nile     ║
║    water cataract dams directly to desert mining complexes.      ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  TRANSFORMER INGREDIENTS                                         ║
║  A) 35 kg Laminated silicon-iron E-I transformer core plates     ║
║     [→ R040 + 3% Silicon from silica slag]                       ║
║     Plates rolled 0.35 mm thick, varnished on one side with      ║
║     shellac/pitch to block inter-laminar eddy currents.          ║
║  B) Primary coil: 200 turns of 2.5 mm dia insulated copper wire  ║
║     [→ R084]. Designed for 240V input at 50 Hz.                 ║
║  C) Secondary coil: 2,000 turns of 0.8 mm dia insulated copper   ║
║     wire [→ R084]. Designed for 2,400V step-up output.           ║
║  D) 5 L Dehydrated transformer insulating oil (mineral/tallow)   ║
║     [→ R051 / R054] to flood tank for cooling and dielectric.    ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  TRANSFORMER WINDING & ASSEMBLY                                  ║
║    1. Stack E-shaped laminations inside wooden coil bobbin.      ║
║    2. Wind primary coil evenly. Wrap with 3 layers of shellacked ║
║       linen cloth dielectric barrier (withstands 5,000V).        ║
║    3. Wind secondary coil in reverse direction in 4 isolated     ║
║       pancake sections to minimize inter-turn voltage stress.    ║
║    4. Interleave I-plates into E-core to close magnetic circuit. ║
║    5. Submerge core in iron tank filled with dry oil.            ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  VOLTAGE RATIO VERIFICATION                                      ║
║    V_s / V_p = N_s / N_p = 2,000 / 200 = 10.0 : 1.0              ║
║    Input: 240V RMS ──► Output: 2,400V RMS. Efficiency: >94%.     ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
