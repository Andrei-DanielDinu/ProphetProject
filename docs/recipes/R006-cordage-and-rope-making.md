# R006 — Cordage and Rope Making (Plant Bast Reverse-Twist)

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R006 · PLANT FIBER CORDAGE & HEAVY ROPE                 ║
║  End product: 20 m of 2-ply cordage (breaking strength 80–120 kg)║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHAT YOU GET                                                    ║
║    Strong, flexible rope for: bowstrings (R013), bellows lashing ║
║    (R026), hauling ore from mines, tying structural timbers.     ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║  A) 5 kg Fresh stems of Wild Flax (Linum), Palm Frond Bast,     ║
║     or Halfa Grass (Stipa tenacissima) [■ TERMINAL]              ║
║  B) Water for retting [→ R000-H]                                 ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PROCESS                                                         ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 1 — WATER RETTING (Bacterial breakdown of pectin)         ║
║    1a. Submerge plant stems in stagnant water pool for 7–10 days.║
║    1b. Anaerobic bacteria consume plant gums and pectins, freeing║
║        the tough cellulose fibers without weakening them.        ║
║    1c. Dry stems in sun for 24 hours.                            ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 2 — SCUTCHING AND COMBING                                  ║
║    2a. Beat dried stems with wooden paddle (R000-I) to shatter   ║
║        brittle inner woody core.                                 ║
║    2b. Pull fibers through flint comb (R005) to separate into    ║
║        silky, long parallel tow fibers.                          ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 3 — TWO-PLY REVERSE-TWIST SPINNING                         ║
║    3a. Take a bundle of fibers (pencil-thick). Fold in half.     ║
║    3b. Twist both strands AWAY from you (clockwise) between thumb║
║        and index finger.                                         ║
║    3c. Cross the top strand over the bottom strand TOWARDS you   ║
║        (counter-clockwise).                                      ║
║    3d. Repeat rhythm: Twist right, wrap left. Twist right, wrap  ║
║        left. Internal opposing torques lock the rope permanently.║
║    3e. Splice in new fibers 3 cm before the end of each bundle.  ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  TENSILE RATINGS                                                 ║
║    • 3 mm cord (2-ply): 30 kg breaking load (bowstrings)         ║
║    • 8 mm rope (2-ply): 120 kg breaking load (hauling, lashing) ║
║    • 20 mm rope (3-strand laid): 800 kg breaking load (cranes)   ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
