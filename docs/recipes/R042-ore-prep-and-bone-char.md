# R042 — Pre-Roasted Ore and Bone Char

## R042-A: Pre-Roasted Hematite Ore

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R042-A · PRE-ROASTED HEMATITE ORE                       ║
║  End product: 10 kg dry, calcined iron ore for smelting          ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHAT YOU GET                                                    ║
║    Dry hematite ore, free of crystal water (FeOOH → Fe₂O₃),    ║
║    thermally cracked (1–3 cm pieces), improved reducibility.    ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║  A) 12 kg raw hematite ore, 3–10 cm chunks   [→ R000-D] TERM. ║
║  B) 4 kg firewood (open fire roasting)        [→ R000-I] TERM. ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  TOOLS: Flat stone surface, 2 kg quartzite hammer               ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PROCESS                                                         ║
║  1. Crush ore with quartzite hammer: 12 kg ore → 1–3 cm pieces. ║
║     Time: 30 min for 12 kg. 1 worker.                           ║
║  2. Build open fire on flat stone. Load ore on top of fire.     ║
║  3. Heat to visible glow (600°C). Hold 1 hour.                  ║
║  4. Colour shift: yellow-brown limonite → red-black hematite.   ║
║     Mass loss: ~15% (water driven off).                          ║
║  5. Cool. Hand-sort: keep dark-red/black heavy pieces.           ║
║     Discard: pale grey/white gangue (rock, no iron).             ║
║  OUTPUT: ~10 kg roasted ore from 12 kg raw.                     ║
║  CYCLE TIME: 90 min.                                            ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

## R042-B: Bone Char (Carburising Catalyst)

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R042-B · BONE CHAR                                       ║
║  End product: 500g bone char (mix of C + Ca₃(PO₄)₂)            ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHAT YOU GET                                                    ║
║    Black granular material: ~10% carbon + ~80% calcium phosphate.║
║    Accelerates pack carburisation (R041) as a catalyst.          ║
║    Also used in water purification (charcoal filter + bone char  ║
║    removes fluoride and heavy metals).                           ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║  A) 1 kg dry cattle/sheep bone                [→ R000-K] TERM. ║
║     Fully dry (boil 2h to remove marrow, then air-dry 3 days).  ║
║  B) Any heat source to 700°C                  [→ R021]          ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PROCESS                                                         ║
║  1. Place dry bones in clay pot with tight lid (small vent hole).║
║  2. Heat in kiln or forge fire to 700°C for 2 hours.            ║
║     Vent hole: white then grey-brown smoke (organic matter).    ║
║  3. When smoke stops: fully charred. Remove, cool 1 hour.       ║
║  4. Grind in stone mortar to powder (<1 mm grain).              ║
║  OUTPUT: ~500g bone char from 1 kg raw bone.                    ║
║  CYCLE TIME: 3 hours + grinding (30 min).                       ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
