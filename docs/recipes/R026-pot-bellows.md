# R026 — Pot Bellows (Forced-Draft Air Pump)

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R026 · POT BELLOWS — DOUBLE-ACTING                      ║
║  End product: 2-unit pot bellows system, 300–600 L/min airflow  ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHAT YOU GET                                                    ║
║    Two clay pot bellows operated alternately by one person,      ║
║    delivering continuous airflow to a smelting tuyere.           ║
║    Without forced air: charcoal fire reaches 700°C maximum.     ║
║    WITH these bellows: 1,100–1,300°C achievable.                ║
║    This is the single most important tool for metallurgy.        ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║                                                                  ║
║  A) 2 × clay pots, 15–20 L capacity each      [→ R020]         ║
║     Dimensions: 40 cm diameter × 35 cm tall. Rim: 30 cm dia.   ║
║     Must be fully fired (not green), crack-free.                ║
║                                                                  ║
║  B) 2 × animal hide membranes, 60 cm dia      [→ R000-K] TERM. ║
║     Fresh or rehydrated rawhide (cattle hide), thinned to 3 mm. ║
║     Must be supple — not dry/stiff. Soak 1 hour in water before ║
║     fitting.                                                     ║
║                                                                  ║
║  C) 2 × clay tuyere pipes (nozzle)            [→ R020]         ║
║     Length: 30 cm. Inner diameter: 3 cm. Outer: 5 cm.          ║
║     Made from: refractory clay (R023) coiled + smoothed.        ║
║     Fire to 900°C before use.                                    ║
║                                                                  ║
║  D) 1 m rawhide lashing strips (hoop clamp)   [→ R000-K] TERM. ║
║     For clamping membrane to pot rim.                            ║
║                                                                  ║
║  E) 2 × wooden handles, 60 cm long            [→ R000-I] TERM. ║
║     Attached to centre of each membrane for pumping.             ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  DIMENSIONS AND ASSEMBLY DIAGRAM                                 ║
║                                                                  ║
║  Side cutaway view of one bellows unit:                          ║
║                                                                  ║
║         HANDLE (pulled up and pushed down)                       ║
║            │                                                     ║
║    ╔═══════╧══════════╗ ← rawhide membrane, 60 cm dia            ║
║    ║                  ║   lashed to rim with hide strips         ║
║    ║   AIR CHAMBER    ║                                          ║
║    ║   (clay pot)     ║                                          ║
║    ║                  ║                                          ║
║    ╚═══════╤══════════╝                                          ║
║            │                                                     ║
║       TUYERE PIPE (3 cm ID clay nozzle, 30 cm long)             ║
║            │                                                     ║
║            └──────────────► to forge/kiln tuyere hole           ║
║                                                                  ║
║  One-way valve: a flap of rawhide inside the pot base covers     ║
║  a 3 cm hole in the base. On DOWN stroke: flap seals, air        ║
║  forces out through tuyere. On UP stroke: flap opens, air enters║
║  from outside through base hole.                                  ║
║                                                                  ║
║  TWO-POT ALTERNATING SYSTEM:                                     ║
║                                                                  ║
║    Bellows 1 ──┐                                                 ║
║                 ├──→ joined clay tuyere → forge                 ║
║    Bellows 2 ──┘                                                 ║
║                                                                  ║
║    Operator pushes Pot 1 down while pulling Pot 2 up.           ║
║    Alternating. Provides CONTINUOUS airflow (no dead spots).     ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PROCESS — CONSTRUCTION                                          ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 1 — PREPARE POTS                                           ║
║    1a. Fire 2 pots (R020) to 900°C.                              ║
║    1b. Chip or drill a 3 cm diameter hole in the base of each   ║
║        pot. Use copper/flint drill or tap with chisel.           ║
║    1c. Chip or drill a 3.5 cm hole in the lower side of each    ║
║        pot (5 cm above base). This is the tuyere pipe fitting.  ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 2 — MAKE ONE-WAY VALVE                                     ║
║    2a. Cut a 6 cm dia circle of thin rawhide.                   ║
║    2b. Punch one hole through its centre (2 mm).                ║
║    2c. Inside the pot: press rawhide circle over the base hole.  ║
║        Secure with one small clay lump over the centre, pressed  ║
║        flat. The rawhide can flap up (inward), not down          ║
║        (outside). This IS the valve.                             ║
║    2d. Test dry: push air through tuyere hole inward → flap      ║
║        presses shut. Pull air outward from tuyere hole → flap    ║
║        lifts open. If not working: adjust clay hold point.       ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 3 — FIT TUYERE PIPE                                        ║
║    3a. Push fired clay tuyere pipe into side hole. Seal gap      ║
║        with wet refractory clay (R023). Allow 24h to set.        ║
║    3b. For two-pot system: extend both tuyere pipes to merge     ║
║        in a Y-junction: form a Y-shaped clay manifold (3 pipes   ║
║        joined at angle). OR: simply aim both tuyere pipes into   ║
║        same tuyere hole in the furnace.                          ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 4 — ATTACH MEMBRANE                                        ║
║    4a. Soak rawhide in water 1 hour (makes pliable).            ║
║    4b. Place pot upright (tuyere pointed toward furnace).        ║
║    4c. Stretch rawhide FLAT across the top rim (open face).     ║
║    4d. Lash tight with hide strips in a cross-pattern. It will  ║
║        shrink drum-tight as it dries — this is correct.          ║
║    4e. While rawhide is still moist: push wooden handle through  ║
║        centre, tie with knot underneath (or thread through a     ║
║        drilled hole in a small wooden washer below hide).        ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 5 — TEST                                                   ║
║    5a. Push handle down: air should exit tuyere.                 ║
║    5b. Pull handle up: air enters from base hole. Listen for     ║
║        flap opening.                                             ║
║    5c. Confirm: hand over tuyere tip — feel steady airflow.     ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  OPERATION                                                       ║
║    Operator kneels between both pots. Right hand pushes Pot 1   ║
║    handle down while left hand pulls Pot 2 up. Alternating.      ║
║    Rate: 1 full stroke per second per pot = 60 strokes/min.     ║
║    Displacement per stroke: 15 L pot volume.                     ║
║    Theoretical flow: 2 × 15 L/s × 60% efficiency = ~18 L/s     ║
║    = 1,080 L/min. Actual (accounting for valve timing): ~600 L/min║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  SKILLS                                                          ║
║    • Rawhide membrane fitting: practice on one pot first.        ║
║    • Alternating rhythm: 20 min practice to get steady.          ║
║    CONSTRUCTION TIME: 2 days (pots pre-fired, then assembly).   ║
║    OPERATOR ENDURANCE: 15-minute shifts (exhausting). Rotate     ║
║    2–3 operators per bellows station.                            ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  UPGRADE PATH                                                    ║
║    Phase 1 (now): 2-pot system, 1 operator, 600 L/min.          ║
║    Phase 2 (once iron tools available): wooden plunger box       ║
║    bellows with iron fittings — 2,000 L/min, 1 operator.        ║
║    Phase 3 (waterwheel available): cam-driven mechanical bellows ║
║    — continuous, unattended airflow.                             ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
