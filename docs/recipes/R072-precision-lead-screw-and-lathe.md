║    Before Maudslay's slide rest (1797), a turner held cutting    ║
║    chisels by hand: results were irregular, wavy, and dependent  ║

║    guided along hand-scraped prismatic ways (R071) and driven by ║
║    a precision lead screw. It cuts perfect cylinders, faces,     ║
║    bores, and cuts exact matching screw threads.                 ║
