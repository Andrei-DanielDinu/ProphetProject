# R164 — Vulcanized Natural Rubber and Pneumatic Tires

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R164 · VULCANIZED RUBBER & AUTOMOTIVE PNEUMATIC TIRES    ║
║  End product: 1 × Steel-Belted Radial Pneumatic Tire (205/55R16) ║
║  Tensile: 25 MPa, Elongation: 500%, Temperature range: -40 to 90°C║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  WHY VULCANIZATION IS CHEMICAL MAGIC (Charles Goodyear, 1839)    ║
║    Raw natural latex rubber (cis-1,4-polyisoprene) is useless:   ║
║    sticky in summer heat, rock-hard and brittle in winter cold.  ║
║    Sulfur vulcanization inserts covalent disulfide (-S-S-)       ║
║    bridges between long polymer chains under heat and pressure.  ║
║    The chains can stretch and slide, but snap back instantly to  ║
║    their original geometry: true elastic rebound!                ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  INGREDIENTS                                                     ║
║  A) 10 kg Raw Natural Rubber (Latex coagulum)                    ║
║     [Tapped from Ficus elastica or Hevea trees; coagulated with  ║
║     0.5% dilute acetic/formic acid, washed, dried into smoked sh]║
║  B) 300g Elemental Sulfur powder (R000 / R053)                   ║
║  C) 3.5 kg Carbon Black Nanopowder (Reinforcing Filler)          ║
║     [Soot collected from thermal partial combustion of heavy     ║
║     petroleum oil R054 in restricted air]: boosts wear by 10×!   ║
║  D) 500g Zinc Oxide (ZnO activator, R000)                        ║
║  E) 200g Stearic acid (from animal tallow R051)                  ║
║  F) 50 m High-tensile brass-plated steel bead wire [→ R084]      ║
║  G) 2 m² Heavy woven nylon tire-cord fabric [→ R163]             ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  MANUFACTURING PROTOCOL                                          ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 1 — MASTICATION & COMPOUNDING (Banbury Internal Mixer)     ║
║    1a. Feed raw rubber sheets into counter-rotating two-roll     ║
║        heavy iron mill (R072): massive mechanical shear tears    ║
║        polymer chains down to workable molecular weight.         ║
║    1b. Add carbon black, zinc oxide, stearic acid, and sulfur.   ║
║        Compound into homogeneous jet-black pliable rubber dough. ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 2 — BUILDING THE "GREEN" TIRE CARCASS                      ║
║    2a. Wrap inner liner sheet (airtight halobutyl/rubber) around ║
║        collapsible cylindrical building drum.                    ║
║    2b. Apply rubberized nylon cord ply layers bias-angled at 15°.║
║    2c. Anchor high-tensile steel bead rings at tire rim edges    ║
║        (locks tire securely onto metal wheel rim).               ║
║    2d. Lay extruded thick tread rubber slab over crown center.   ║
║    2e. Collapse drum: yields un-vulcanized "green" soft tire.    ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STEP 3 — VULCANIZATION PRESS & MOLDING                          ║
║    3a. Insert green tire into segmented heated steel tire mold.  ║
║    3b. Inflate internal curing bladder with 15 bar high-pressure ║
║        steam (R070): forces rubber out against steel mold walls, ║
║        forming intricate tread rain-sipes and sidewall lettering!║
║    3c. Cure at 145°C for 22 minutes:                             ║
║        Disulfide cross-links lock all components into a single   ║
║        monolithic, wear-resistant, high-grip elastomeric matrix! ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  BURST PRESSURE TEST: Inflate with water to 12 bar (175 psi) —   ║
║  withstands 4× rated operational pressure with zero leakage!     ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
