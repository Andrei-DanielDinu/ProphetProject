# R100 — Semiconductor Silicon (Czochralski Monocrystalline Ingot)

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  RECIPE R100 · ELECTRONIC GRADE SILICON & CZOCHRALSKI INGOT      ║
║  End product: 1 × Monocrystalline Silicon Ingot (150 mm dia)     ║
║  Purity: 99.9999999% ("Nine Nines" / 9N), Dislocation-free       ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  PURPOSE IN RECURSIVE CHAIN                                      ║
║    The physical semiconductor wafer substrate upon which:        ║
║    • Planar microchips & microprocessors are fabricated [→ R101] ║
║    • Photovoltaic solar cells are manufactured                   ║
║    • High-frequency RF diodes and power MOSFETs operate          ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  THE PURITY CASCADE                                              ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STAGE 1: METALLURGICAL GRADE SILICON (MGS, 98% Si)              ║
║    1a. Melt pure quartz pebbles (SiO₂, R000-F) + crushed pure    ║
║        charcoal (C, R025) in an electric submerged-arc furnace:  ║
║        SiO₂ + 2 C ──► Si + 2 CO↑  at 1,900°C.                    ║
║    1b. Tap molten metallurgical silicon (purity ~98.5%).         ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STAGE 2: CHEMICAL PURIFICATION (Siemens Process, 9N)            ║
║    2a. Grind MGS to powder. React with anhydrous HCl gas (R053)  ║
║        at 300°C: Si + 3 HCl ──► SiHCl₃ (Trichlorosilane) + H₂.   ║
║    2b. Trichlorosilane is a liquid boiling at 31.8°C.            ║
║        Fractionally distill through a 20-tray quartz column:     ║
║        All metal impurities (Fe, Al, B, P) have different boiling║
║        points and are discarded with 99.9999999% separation.    ║
║    2c. Decompose pure SiHCl₃ gas with H₂ over thin glowing       ║
║        silicon filament rods at 1,150°C:                         ║
║        SiHCl₃ + H₂ ──► Si (Hyper-pure Polycrystalline) + 3 HCl.  ║
║                                                                  ║
║  STAGE 3: CZOCHRALSKI CRYSTAL GROWTH                             ║
║    3a. Melt 20 kg hyper-pure polycrystalline silicon in a high-  ║
║        purity fused silica quartz crucible (R060) at 1,425°C     ║
║        inside an induction-heated, inert Argon vacuum chamber.   ║
║    3b. Add 5 mg high-purity Boron (P-type dopant, 10¹⁵ atoms/cm³).║
║    3c. Lower a single-crystal silicon seed oriented in the <100> ║
║        crystallographic plane into the surface of the melt.      ║
║    3d. Begin Dash Necking technique: withdraw seed at 4 mm/min   ║
║        to grow a 3 mm thin neck: all crystal dislocations shoot  ║
║        out to the surface, leaving the core dislocation-free!    ║
║    3e. Slowly lower pull rate to 1.0 mm/min while rotating seed  ║
║        at 15 RPM and rotating crucible counter at 5 RPM:         ║
║        Ingot flares out into a perfect 150 mm diameter cylinder. ║
║    3f. Cool slowly over 10 hours.                                ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  SLICING AND POLISHING: Cut ingot with diamond wire saw into     ║
║  625 μm thick wafers. Polish with colloidal silica slurry on     ║
║  polyurethane laps to sub-nanometer surface roughness (CMP).     ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
