# Become the Uomo Universale

*The broad, capable human: not omniscient, but able to learn across domains, connect ideas, build useful systems, and teach others.*

## The candidate standard

A credible candidate is not selected by charisma or self-proclamation. They demonstrate:

- **Learning agility:** acquire unfamiliar concepts, test understanding, and update beliefs.
- **Scientific judgment:** distinguish observation, theory, evidence, estimate, and speculation.
- **Practical competence:** make, repair, measure, grow, organize, and troubleshoot—not merely explain.
- **Systems thinking:** see dependencies, feedback loops, constraints, and unintended consequences.
- **Communication:** explain ideas at the learner’s level, listen, negotiate, and write clear procedures.
- **Teaching and succession:** develop people who can teach without the founder.
- **Personal sustainability:** maintain sleep, health, emotional regulation, relationships, and realistic pacing.
- **Ethical leadership:** welcome scrutiny, respect consent, share credit, and accept correction.

## The learning loop

1. Map the problem and prerequisites.
2. Learn the simplest reliable explanation.
3. Demonstrate it with a small experiment or prototype.
4. Record the method, measurements, failures, and uncertainty.
5. Teach someone else to reproduce it.
6. Ask that learner to teach a third person.
7. Revise the curriculum based on errors and local context.

## A practical curriculum

| Domain | Foundational capability | Evidence of competence |
|---|---|---|
| Numeracy and measurement | arithmetic, ratios, geometry, units, estimation | repeatable measurements; catches implausible results |
| Natural science | observation, controlled tests, causal reasoning | useful experiment log; replication |
| Materials and making | tools, joining, heat, wear, repair | makes and repairs useful objects safely |
| Food and health | nutrition, sanitation, agriculture, first aid | improves a measurable local outcome without overclaiming |
| Energy and mechanics | force, work, machines, power | builds and maintains a simple mechanism |
| Communication | explanation, negotiation, documentation | teaches a skill to learners with different backgrounds |
| Institutions | accounting, rules, conflict resolution, incentives | creates a fair process that functions under stress |
| Self-management | rest, reflection, emotional regulation, boundaries | sustains work without burnout or identity fusion |

## Candidate readiness review

Use a portfolio, not a personality cult. Review practical demonstrations, teaching outcomes, peer feedback, decision logs, and how the candidate handles disagreement. No single person should be the sole judge of readiness.

## Keep the mission human

A civilization is not a machine whose people are expendable inputs. Progress is meaningful only if it improves lives, protects agency, and remains open to correction.
