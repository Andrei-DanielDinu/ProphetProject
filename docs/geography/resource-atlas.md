## 1. Regional Mineral and Energy Mapping

| Material | Key Geological Deposits | Extraction Route | Primary Transport Mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Copper** | Eastern Desert (Wadi Dara), Sinai (Maghara, Timna) | Surface trenches & shafts | Donkey caravan to Nile or Red Sea |
| **Gold** | Eastern Desert quartz veins (Wadi Hammamat, Allaqi) | Hard rock crushing & panning | Desert fortress convoys |
| **Limestone** | Tura, Massara, Middle Egypt cliffs | Open quarrying with bronze chisels | River barges on the Nile |
| **Granite & Basalt** | Aswan (First Cataract), Gebel Qatrani (Fayum) | Pounding with dolerite balls & wedges | Heavy timber river barges during flood |
| **Natron (Salt)** | Wadi Natrun evaporite lakes | Surface harvesting of crust | Overland pack caravans to Delta |
| **Cedar Timber** | Mount Lebanon (Byblos corridor) | Felling and mountain skidways | Maritime sailing merchantmen |
| **Tin** | Anatolia (Kestel), Central Asia, or Cornwall | Stream alluvial panning | Trans-Mediterranean sailing convoys |
| **Iron Ore** | Aswan oolitic ironstone, Bahariya Oasis | Open-pit quarrying | Nile barges and rail corridors (Phase 3+) |
| **Petroleum & Gas** | Gulf of Suez, Western Desert, Nile Delta | Surface seep collection $\to$ deep rotary drilling | Pipelines and maritime tankers (Phase 5+) |
| **Uranium** | Eastern Desert granites (Gattar, El Erediya) | Radiometric prospecting & leaching | Armored radioactive transport casks (Phase 6+) |
