# Geography: Trade Routes, Harbors, and Chokepoints

> "A road is an energy-saving device. An engineered route reduces the friction of distance."

---

## 1. Terrestrial Corridors

1. **Wadi Hammamat**: The primary dry canyon connecting Koptos on the Nile with the Red Sea at Quseir. Provides direct access to greywacke quarries, gold mines, and Red Sea seafaring.
2. **The Ways of Horus (Northern Sinai Coast)**: The fortified coastal military and trade highway linking the Eastern Delta (Pelusium) with Canaan and the Levant, secured by regular fortified water wells.
3. **The Darb el-Arbain (Forty Days Road)**: The desert oasis track connecting Asyut on the Nile to the Western Desert oases (Kharga, Dakhla) and Sudan.

---

## 2. Ports and Maritime Terminals

- **Red Sea Ports (Wadi al-Jarf, Mersa Gawasis, Quseir)**: Ancient artificial harbors with rock-cut boat storage galleries and stone anchor caches, enabling seasonal expeditions down to the land of Punt (Eritrea/Somalia) for incense and resins.
- **Mediterranean Terminals (Avaris, Pelusium, later Alexandria)**: Sheltered river-mouth estuaries providing safe anchorage for sea-going cedar vessels from Phoenicia and Cyprus.
